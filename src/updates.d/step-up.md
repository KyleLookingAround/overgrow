# Take the plot

- **The step up.** Win the garden's year and take a plot at the allotment, or stay a while.
- **The zoom-out.** Your garden shrinks into one tile among twelve.
- **Plan, don't dig.** Set your plot's care, its mix and its feed, and watch the numbers move.
- **Neighbours.** Eleven plots, each kept as well as its household has time.

History: the garden became a plot, and the game its second level: an allotment of twelve plots, the player's planned from further away.
