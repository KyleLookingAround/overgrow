# A gentler start

- **One thing at a time.** Numbers, tabs and dials appear as you first get a say over them.
- **The first plan.** The game opens paused on the first plan; sow it or let the gardener choose.
- **The goal bar.** It names what's holding the garden back from the allotment, and why.
- **Show all details.** Tick it at the foot of the Garden tab to see every number at once.

History: The garden now shows each instrument only once the player can act on it, with one sign when something new appears and a "Show all details" setting. The game opens paused on the first plan's card, the goal bar names the requirement holding the garden back, the Shed tab shows the beer trap, and the Explain card is shorter.
