# The allotment's first season

- **Neighbours.** Eleven households garden as their lives allow, a face each.
- **A second plot.** Take on the neglected plot, or let a helper work it for a share.
- **The trough.** A dry spell leaves the back of the queue short: vote on the rota.
- **The swap shed.** Swap your surplus for what the household is short of.

History: the allotment filled with people: neighbours on their plots, a second plot with a helper who reports less than they take, a shared trough and its first vote, pests from next door and a swap shed.
