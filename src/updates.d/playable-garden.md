# The playable garden

- **The shed sells.** Beer traps, a hose, nematodes, a compost bin, a cold frame and a second butt, each a trade.
- **Dig more beds.** Once every bed is in use, dig another from the lawn: more to eat, sell and do.
- **Winter crops.** Garlic, broad beans, onions, winter salad, kale, leeks and a green manure keep beds busy.
- **A goal you can act on.** The goal bar names the next thing to do, and the year ends with a card.

History: the garden became a game: buy from the shed, dig beds from the lawn, sow for the winter, and reach the allotment offer after a year, with the goal bar naming the next step.
