# The garden's whole year

- **A budget to plan.** Seed, new beds and the shed's kit now come out of a weekly garden budget.
- **Big buys to save for.** A greenhouse, a fruit cage, raised beds, a rain tank, and hens that lay.
- **No bed left idle.** When a bed empties, a card suggests what to sow next, in one tap.

History: The garden gets a weekly budget and things worth saving for: a greenhouse, a fruit cage, raised beds, a rainwater tank and three hens. Digging a bed is a week's work, and an empty bed asks what goes in next.
