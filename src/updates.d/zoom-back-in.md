# The first zoom back in

- **Slugs next door.** In your plot's first summer, slugs from a neglected plot get into your garden.
- **Go down.** Dive back into your garden and fix it before the deadline.
- **Rescued.** A rescue in time marks your plot and wins the neighbours' goodwill.
- **Send someone.** Or pay an adviser to sort it, for half the reward.
