# A winter worth playing

- **Winter jobs.** Rake leaves, plant bare-root fruit, and choose to dig or not.
- **Smaller buys.** A fork, cloches, a propagator and a bee hotel, each a real trade.
- **Your first year.** The anniversary shows what the garden did and what's short.
- **One tap.** The goal bar's button does the step or opens the Shed at it.

History: the autumn and winter became a season of jobs and choices, the Shed gained mid-priced kit and a purse line, and the garden's first year ends with a card.
