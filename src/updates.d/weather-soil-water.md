# Weather, soil and water

- **Real weather.** A southern English year falls on the garden: rain, sun and frosty mornings.
- **Living soil.** Beds darken in the rain and pale as they dry; bare soil slowly loses its goodness.
- **What's in the ground.** Tap a bed to see its moisture, organic matter, nutrients and health.

History: the garden got its weather, from rain showers crossing it to frost on the lawn, and soil in every bed that wets, dries and slowly changes.
