# Pests, wildlife and why things happen

- **Slugs and aphids.** Slugs creep out on wet nights; aphids crowd the beans in June.
- **Blight.** Muggy summer spells bring blight to potatoes and tomatoes.
- **Flowers help.** Marigolds bring bees and ladybirds; set the pest policy in the Garden tab.
- **Tap to explain.** Tap any effect, badge or number to see what happened and why.

History: Slugs, aphids and blight arrive in the garden, with a pest policy the gardener follows (leave, pick, trap or treat) and marigolds that bring bees and ladybirds. Every effect so far opens an Explain card saying what happened, how it works and the source.
