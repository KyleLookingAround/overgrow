# A box you find, a midwinter to play

- **The box, found.** The honesty box's choice comes as soon as there's a surplus, and its tick sits with the harvest.
- **Force chicory.** In January, pale leaves under the pots, or wash the pots for slugs: one or the other.
- **Numbers with words.** The step-up card and the shop say what their figures mean.

History: the honesty box's choice came with the first surplus, midwinter gained a choice between forcing chicory and washing the pots, and the figures got words.
