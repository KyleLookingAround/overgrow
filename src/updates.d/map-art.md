# A garden you can read at a glance

- **The ground is ground.** Mowing stripes, a hedge, a fence, a back door and gravel paths that puddle.
- **Plants that grow.** Each crop has its own shape, fuller when ready, wilting and yellowing when short.
- **Light and seasons.** Warm dawns, frost on the beds, snow in freezing rain, leaves and blossom.
- **Small life.** The gardener walks, the hens step, the bees have wings, and a robin visits in winter.

History: the map had its first art pass, with a style kit every level's map now draws with: textured ground and structures, crops with a silhouette a stage, the light and the seasons drawn, and the garden's people and creatures given a little character.
