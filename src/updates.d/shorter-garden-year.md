# A shorter garden year

- **A faster day.** The garden's day passes twice as fast, so a year fits one evening.
- **Quiet nights.** When nothing needs you, the night passes quickly under a moon.
- **8× speed.** A new top speed for the long waits.

History: the garden's year takes about 53 minutes at 1× instead of two and a half hours: its day is twice as fast, quiet nights pass four times faster, and there's an 8× speed.
