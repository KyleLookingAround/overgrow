# A garden that pays its way by steps

- **A money ladder.** The goal bar names the next thing to save for, and the gap.
- **Big buys in steps.** A hen house before the hens, a lean-to, one bush before the cage.
- **Sell when short.** Keep the honesty box stocked; the glut card shows what each pays.
- **Midwinter jobs.** Pruning, seed potatoes, clean pots, windowsill salad, hen care.

History: the big buys came in steps a garden can save for, the goal bar named the next one with its price, the honesty box could be kept stocked year-round, and midwinter gained real jobs; new things unfold one a day.
