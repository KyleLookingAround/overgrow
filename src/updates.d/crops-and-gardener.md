# Things grow, and the gardener does the work

- **Crops grow.** Six crops sprout, grow, wilt in a drought and blacken in a frost.
- **A head start.** Bed 1 opens with last autumn's salad leaves, ready to cut this week.
- **The gardener works.** Watch them sow, water, pick and carry, four hours a day.
- **Plan the beds.** The Garden tab sets what to sow, when, and when to water.

History: The garden grows food, starting with a bed of overwintered salad leaves. The gardener follows the player's plan within their hours, the household eats what's picked, the surplus sells at an honesty box, and the compost heap counts on the carbon dial.
