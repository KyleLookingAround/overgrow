# One map, and the zoom is the speed

- **Zoom in and out.** Pinch, scroll or use + and −, from one bed out to the garden fence, and from one plot out to the whole allotment.
- **The zoom sets the pace.** Zoomed out, the days go faster. There are no speed buttons now, just Pause.
- **Skip ahead.** When nothing needs you, a chip at the top offers to skip to what's next. It stops if something comes up.
- **Your garden in your plot.** Zoom into your plot at the allotment to see the garden you left, with a gold tag on it.
- **Your neighbours' plots.** Zoom into any plot to see its beds.

History: The garden and the allotment on one camera: the zoom sets the pace instead of speed buttons, skips run to what's next, your garden sits inside your plot, and every neighbour's plot opens in detail.
