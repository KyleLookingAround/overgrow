# The garden keeps giving

- **Compost feeds back.** Compost returns the phosphorus and potassium your crops took.
- **Years, not months.** The beds keep cropping into year three and beyond.

History: the garden no longer runs out of phosphorus and potassium by its third year: crop residues go to the heap, compost carries their nutrients back, and the beds start as a long-kept garden's.
