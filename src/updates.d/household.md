# The gardener's job and the weekly shop

- **A day job.** The gardener works weekdays, which is why the garden gets four hours a day.
- **Payday and the shop.** Friday's pay fills the purse; the shop buys what the garden didn't grow.
- **Groceries saved.** What the household eats from the garden is food it didn't have to buy.
- **The shop's footprint.** A bag under the carbon dial: what the shop's food carried, never added.

History: The gardener now has a weekday job and brings home the weekly shop on Friday with their pay; the Kitchen tab shows last week's pay, shop, rest of life and groceries saved, the shop food's footprint sits under the carbon dial, the kitchen asks for one person's veg, and passers-by at the honesty box take the best first.
