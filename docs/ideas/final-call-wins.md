# Easy wins from Final Call

Written 29 Sep 2026 from the brief `docs/briefs/final-call-wins.md`. Not agreed yet: this folder holds ideas until the owner picks. The pick is made on a page linked from the `needs-owner` issue, with each row's recommendation ticked by default; after 12 hours with no answer the defaults stand. The coordinator folds the chosen wins into the parts' briefs.

**How to read it.** Each row is one win: what it is, where it lives in Final Call (paths under its `src/`, `tools/` and `docs/`), why it fits Overgrow or how it must change for a map-first, Preact and PixiJS game with light and dark themes, where it lands (a part of the founding spec's "The first roadmap", the runbook, or after the slice), its size (S under an hour of a session, M a few hours, L a part of its own) and a recommendation:

- **Now:** cheap, and the sooner the better. A playbook line goes straight into this PR (marked *in this PR*); anything under `src/`, `tools/`, `.github/` or the project notes is queued for the coordinator, because the parts own that code while they build.
- **With its part:** goes into that part's brief.
- **Later:** worth having, after the slice or when its trigger comes.
- **No:** see the end.

"Easy" means the idea and its proof exist in Final Call and the cost here is small. Where a win is valuable but not easy, the row says so.

**What Overgrow already has** (nothing to do): a 40 px touch token and a `layout` check that every button on a touch page is at least that (`src/ui/styles/tokens.css`, `tools/checks/layout.mjs`); `viewport-fit=cover`; "measure before you fix" and the six test sizes in the `feature` playbook; briefs checked by `tools/brief.mjs`; the Description check that strips a trailing footer; one file per entry and Catch up; the fresh review before opening; a rule to say why past twice the estimate; the `needs-owner` queue with a 12-hour default; the cheaper model for research; `graph.mjs`; reduced-motion and a 5,000-node scene run in the `scene` check (further than Final Call went). Final Call has little to give on accessibility beyond keyboard shortcuts (W16): its reduced-motion rules cover a few animations, and Overgrow's map rule "never colour alone" has no Final Call source.

### Playbook lines in this PR (W14, W38–W40)

These went straight into the playbooks (not `balance`), and change no check or tool:
