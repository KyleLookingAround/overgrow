# The map and the page's shell

The page (`src/ui/App.tsx`): a top bar (`src/ui/TopBar.tsx`), the map filling the rest (`src/ui/MapView.tsx`, drawn by `src/ui/map/renderer.ts` and `src/ui/map/draw.ts`), and the panel beside it or below it (`src/ui/Panel.tsx`, with its Garden and Kitchen tabs in `src/ui/GardenTab.tsx` and `src/ui/KitchenTab.tsx`), laid out by `src/ui/styles/page.css` with every colour and size in `src/ui/styles/tokens.css`, all drawn from what the clock loop's view shows. The UI changes the game only by sending commands.

- **The renderer** (`src/ui/map/renderer.ts`): PixiJS on WebGL, and Pixi's own Canvas 2D renderer where WebGL is missing (`docs/decisions/ADR-2026-09-28-webgl-map.md`). The ground (every node with a box) is drawn once into one Graphics and redrawn only when the graph's `rev`, a bed plot being dug (`isDug()`, which the panel shares), the size or the colours change; the butt's water, each dug bed's soil (paling towards `--map-bed-dry` as its surface dries and darkening towards `--map-bed-wet` when soaked, `wetness()`, and darkening at once while rain falls on it, drying over three hours after, `surfaceWet()`) and the frost's rime are redrawn each frame, interpolated between the two snapshots, and rain falls as a pool of particles while the sim says it rains (`docs/systems/weather.md`); under `prefers-reduced-motion` the rain stops falling but stays shown; people are particles from one texture, one for each person (their activities grouped once a snapshot), placed each frame where their latest-started activity puts them at the view time, with what they carry (a can, a basket, a bucket) drawn beside them (`drawItem()`); each dug bed's crop is drawn each frame from its `crop` lever (`drawCrop()`: drills before the shoots, plants growing to their size in rows by crop, yellowing and drooping as the water stress coefficient falls, blackened by frost and fading back over a week for potatoes, and ripe tomatoes, radishes and bean pods showing), and the honesty box on its post by the side gate; a night layer darkens with the sun (`src/ui/map/daylight.ts`, day length at 51.5° N from the date, cosmetic). Shapes are drawn in CSS pixels through a camera that fits the level on the canvas, so they stay crisp at any size. A tap selects the place under it in the panel. `src/ui/MapView.tsx` mounts it, resizes it, and follows `prefers-color-scheme` and `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **The style** (`src/ui/map/draw.ts`): the owner's pick, flat, top-down and soft (`docs/specs/overgrow/art-styles.html`, style A): rounded shapes with no outlines, soft shadows, greens, soil browns and cream. Each node kind has its drawing; an undug bed plot is grass with a dashed edge. Every colour is a `--map-*` token in `src/ui/styles/tokens.css`, light and dark, read by `src/ui/map/palette.ts`.
- **The top bar** (`src/ui/TopBar.tsx`): the level, the date and hour with the air's temperature (hidden below 480 px wide, where it would push the speeds onto a third row), the money, the carbon dial (a needle on a log scale with the kg beside it, so it isn't colour alone) and pause with 1×, 2× and 4× as pressed buttons. The speed shown is the last one set, even when the map is a step behind.
- **The panel** (`src/ui/Panel.tsx`): tabs along its head, as pressed buttons (Garden and Kitchen, each shown once it has something in it; Shed and Goals come with part 6). The Garden tab (`src/ui/GardenTab.tsx`) has the gardener's card (the job in hand, from their latest-started activity, and a meter of the day's hours left), the plan (for each dug bed its crop and status, what to sow or the rotation, and from when; and the moisture below which the gardener waters; each change a `plan` command; the pest policy joins it in part 5), then the garden's places as buttons and what the selected one holds (a bed's crop and quality too) (`src/ui/format.ts`, concise UK English with units): for a bed or the lawn, its moisture (the share of its water for roots, or "Full, draining"), organic matter, nitrate, phosphorus, potassium, soil health and the carbon in its soil. The Kitchen tab (`src/ui/KitchenTab.tsx`) shows the day's ask by group with a meter of what met it at the last meal, the last week's share, what's in the kitchen and the honesty box, and what's been picked, eaten, sold (and for how much) and wasted. On a portrait phone it's a sheet under the map, at most 45 % of the height, that folds to its heading; beside the map on tablets and wide screens, and beside a narrower one on a phone on its side (`src/ui/styles/page.css`). 
- **Access.** Every button is at least 40 px, every panel works by keyboard with a visible focus ring, the map's places are all in the panel's list, and the map is labelled for screen readers. The `layout` check holds the sizes and the keyboard; the `scene` check holds the drawing, the interpolation, the reduced-motion jump, the weather (rain only while it rains and still under reduced motion, frost, the soil's colour), the Canvas 2D fallback and a repeating seeded screenshot; the `garden` check holds the crops and the gardener drawn, and the tabs working.
