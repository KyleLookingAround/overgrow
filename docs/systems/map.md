# The map and the page's shell

The page (`src/ui/App.tsx`): a top bar (`src/ui/TopBar.tsx`), the map filling the rest (`src/ui/MapView.tsx`, drawn by `src/ui/map/renderer.ts` and `src/ui/map/draw.ts`), and the panel beside it or below it (`src/ui/Panel.tsx`, with its Garden and Kitchen tabs in `src/ui/GardenTab.tsx` and `src/ui/KitchenTab.tsx`), laid out by `src/ui/styles/page.css` with every colour and size in `src/ui/styles/tokens.css`, all drawn from what the clock loop's view shows. The UI changes the game only by sending commands. Over the map sit the badges, the Explain card and the notices (`docs/systems/explain.md`), the first plan's card and the goal bar, one card at a time (`docs/systems/unfolding.md`), and the pests and wildlife are drawn by `src/ui/map/life.ts`. The shell follows the UI rules taken from Final Call (`docs/decisions/ADR-2026-09-29-ui-from-final-call.md`).

- **The renderer** (`src/ui/map/renderer.ts`): PixiJS on WebGL, and Pixi's own Canvas 2D renderer where WebGL is missing (`docs/decisions/ADR-2026-09-28-webgl-map.md`). The ground (every node with a box) is drawn once into one Graphics and redrawn only when the graph's `rev`, a bed plot being dug (`isDug()`, which the panel shares), the size or the colours change; the butt's water, each dug bed's soil (paling towards `--map-bed-dry` as its surface dries and darkening towards `--map-bed-wet` when soaked, `wetness()`, and darkening at once while rain falls on it, drying over three hours after, `surfaceWet()`) and the frost's rime are redrawn each frame, interpolated between the two snapshots, and rain falls as a pool of particles while the sim says it rains (`docs/systems/weather.md`); under `prefers-reduced-motion` the rain stops falling but stays shown; people are particles from one texture, one for each person (their activities grouped once a snapshot), placed each frame where their latest-started activity puts them at the view time, with what they carry (a can, a basket, a bucket, the weekly shop's two bags) drawn beside them (`drawItem()`), and not drawn while they're `away` at work beyond the gate (the gardener walks out to the gate at 08:30 on a working day and in from it to the kitchen at 17:30, `docs/systems/gardener.md`); each dug bed's crop is drawn each frame from its `crop` lever (`drawCrop()`: drills before the shoots, plants growing to their size in rows by crop, yellowing and drooping as the water stress coefficient falls, blackened by frost and fading back over a week for potatoes, and ripe tomatoes, radishes and bean pods showing), and the honesty box on its post by the side gate; the pests and wildlife are drawn from the sim's populations with cosmetic scatter seeded from the game's seed (`src/ui/map/life.ts`: slugs creeping out after dark and their slime, aphid clusters, bees, ladybirds, the cat; blight browning and bites out of nibbled plants, marigolds and a bed's border in `drawCrop()` and `drawLive()`), the gardener's torch lights the ground on the dusk patrol, a ring pulses round the Explain card's place, and a night layer darkens with the sun (`src/ui/map/daylight.ts`, day length at 51.5° N from the date, cosmetic). Shapes are drawn in CSS pixels through a camera that fits the level on the canvas, so they stay crisp at any size. A tap on a creature opens its Explain card (`creatureAt()`); otherwise it selects the place under it in the panel. `src/ui/MapView.tsx` mounts it, resizes it, and follows `prefers-color-scheme` and `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **The style** (`src/ui/map/draw.ts`): the owner's pick, flat, top-down and soft (`docs/specs/overgrow/art-styles.html`, style A): rounded shapes with no outlines, soft shadows, greens, soil browns and cream. Each node kind has its drawing; an undug bed plot is grass with a dashed edge. Every colour is a `--map-*` token in `src/ui/styles/tokens.css`, light and dark, read by `src/ui/map/palette.ts`.
- **The top bar** (`src/ui/TopBar.tsx`): the level, the date and hour with the air's temperature, the money, the carbon dial (a needle on a log scale with the kg beside it, so it isn't colour alone; the money and the dial open their Explain cards) and pause with 1×, 2×, 4×, 8× and 16× as a segmented control, a moon on the pressed one while a quiet night passes quickly (`src/ui/quiet-night.ts`); the temperature, the money and the dial each show once they've unfolded (`docs/systems/unfolding.md`). It steps by its own width with container queries (the owner's win W2): below 600 px inside its padding the temperature goes, below 1024 px 4×, 8× and 16× share one button that cycles them, below 700 px pause and every speed fold into one button that shows the speed and cycles it, a tap on a paused game resuming (W11), below 480 px the type steps down and the shop's footprint leaves the dial, and below 400 px the level's name gives way once the money has unfolded, so the bar is one row (at most 60 px) at every size. On a sheet layout (below) the folded speed button leaves the bar for the map's foot, in thumb reach (`src/ui/App.tsx`, `.speed-pill`). The speed shown is the last one set, even when the map is a step behind.
- **The panel** (`src/ui/Panel.tsx`): tabs along its head, as pressed buttons (Garden, Shed and Kitchen, each shown once it has unfolded, `src/ui/ShedTab.tsx` the Shed's; Goals comes later). The Garden tab (`src/ui/GardenTab.tsx`) has the gardener's card (the job in hand, from their latest-started activity, and a meter of the day's hours left), the plan (for each dug bed its crop and status, what to sow or the rotation, and from when; and the moisture below which the gardener waters; each change a `plan` command; and for each pest the garden's crops draw, the pest policy as a `policy` command, and a border of marigolds along each bed's edge), then the garden's places as buttons and what the selected one holds (a bed's crop and quality too) (`src/ui/format.ts`, concise UK English with units): for a bed or the lawn, its moisture (the share of its water for roots, or "Full, draining"), organic matter, nitrate, phosphorus, potassium, soil health and the carbon in its soil, its slugs, aphids, blight, what pests have taken and its border, and what happened there in the last week; each number with an Explain card opens it. The Kitchen tab (`src/ui/KitchenTab.tsx`) shows the day's ask by group with a meter of what met it at the last meal, the last week's share, what's in the kitchen and the honesty box, and what's been picked, eaten, sold (and for how much) and wasted. On phones and tablets held upright it's a sheet under the map with three resting heights in CSS (`docs/decisions/ADR-2026-09-29-ui-overhaul.md`): peek (its head only), half (44 % of the screen, where it rests) and tall (78 %, always leaving 160 px of map), cycled by its button, a tab tap opening a folded sheet; a card over the map folds it to its head while the card is up, and the button says so. It's beside the map on tablets on their side, laptops and up (360 px, 400 px from 1920 px and 440 px from 2560 px, its text never wider than 40 rem), and beside a narrower map on a phone on its side (`src/ui/styles/page.css`): screen-width media queries choose only the sheet or the side panel, and the map, the panel and the top bar are size containers the rest steps by; the page keeps the safe areas clear on every side (`env(safe-area-inset-*)`), the top bar at the top and the sheet's foot, the goal bar and the card's footer at the bottom. The map's share of the screen has a floor per device class, held by the `layout` check. The tabs lead with one headline number each (the gardener's hours left, the share of the day's ask met) and the Shed reads as a shop, the next thing to save for first and each offer a row with its price.
- **Over the map** (`src/ui/MapView.tsx`), from the bottom: the badges and the small rewards, the goal bar at the foot (one pill with the ring, the verb and the one accent button, which stays under a notice and gives way to a card), the notices at the top (one line, never wider than the map, two lines at most until tapped), the fullscreen button at the top right corner (`src/ui/Fullscreen.tsx`, only where the browser has the Fullscreen API; iPhone Safari gets the manifest and the Apple meta tags in `index.html` and `public/` instead, so "Add to Home Screen" opens without chrome), and one card docked away from its place (`data-dock` on the layer: at the top when the place is in the map's lower half). Every colour, size, radius, shadow and step of the type and spacing scales is a token (`src/ui/styles/tokens.css`): text at least 4.5:1 and control borders at least 3:1 on their surface in both schemes.
- **Access.** Every tap target is at least 44 px on touch and 40 px with a mouse, every panel works by keyboard with a visible focus ring, the map's places are all in the panel's list, the map is labelled for screen readers, every tap presses its control, and reduced motion shows stills. The `layout` check holds the sizes and the keyboard; the `scene` check holds the drawing, the interpolation, the reduced-motion jump, the weather (rain only while it rains and still under reduced motion, frost, the soil's colour), the Canvas 2D fallback and a repeating seeded screenshot; the `garden` check holds the crops and the gardener drawn, and the tabs working; the `explain` check holds the card, the badges and a creature's tap.

## The playable garden, round two

- **The big buys** are drawn where they work (`drawNode()` and `drawSites()` in `src/ui/map/draw.ts`): the greenhouse's glass and glazing bars over its border, the hens' run with their house (empty until the hens are bought) and however many are in wandering it by day (a cosmetic loop from the clock) and in the house at night, the fruit cage's bushes under a net with red fruit showing as it ripens, raised beds' timber boards, the rainwater tank against the house past the shed, and (round four) the lean-to's clear panels and pots against the house wall and the blackcurrant bush by the heap. The colours are tokens (`--map-timber`, `--map-tank`, `--map-run`, `--map-hen-house`, `--map-hen`, `--map-comb`, `--map-net`, `--map-berry`).
- **The small rewards** (`src/ui/juice.ts`, drawn by `src/ui/Juice.tsx` over the map like the badges): a purchase pulses where the thing now stands (the buy's `buying` effect is at its place, `placeOf()` in `src/sim/shed.ts`), a sale sends a coin up from the honesty box towards the purse, the first harvest bursts in leaves over its bed, and a sowing drops seed on its bed. Each lasts 1.6 s; under reduced motion each is a still.
