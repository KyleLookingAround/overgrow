# The map and the page's shell

The page (`src/ui/App.tsx`): a top bar (`src/ui/TopBar.tsx`), the map filling the rest (`src/ui/MapView.tsx`, drawn by `src/ui/map/renderer.ts` and `src/ui/map/draw.ts`), and the panel beside it or below it (`src/ui/Panel.tsx`, with its Garden and Kitchen tabs in `src/ui/GardenTab.tsx` and `src/ui/KitchenTab.tsx`), laid out by `src/ui/styles/page.css` with every colour and size in `src/ui/styles/tokens.css`, all drawn from what the clock loop's view shows. The UI changes the game only by sending commands. Over the map sit the badges, the Explain card and the notices (`docs/systems/explain.md`), the first plan's card and the goal bar, one card at a time (`docs/systems/unfolding.md`), and the pests and wildlife are drawn by `src/ui/map/life.ts`. The shell follows the UI rules taken from Final Call (`docs/decisions/ADR-2026-09-29-ui-from-final-call.md`).

- **The renderer** (`src/ui/map/renderer.ts`): PixiJS on WebGL, and Pixi's own Canvas 2D renderer where WebGL is missing (`docs/decisions/ADR-2026-09-28-webgl-map.md`). The ground (every node with a box) is drawn once into one Graphics and redrawn only when the graph's `rev`, a bed plot being dug (`isDug()`, which the panel shares), the size or the colours change; the butt's water, each dug bed's soil (paling towards `--map-bed-dry` as its surface dries and darkening towards `--map-bed-wet` when soaked, `wetness()`, and darkening at once while rain falls on it, drying over three hours after, `surfaceWet()`) and the frost's rime are redrawn each frame, interpolated between the two snapshots, and rain falls as a pool of particles while the sim says it rains (`docs/systems/weather.md`); under `prefers-reduced-motion` the rain stops falling but stays shown; people are particles from one texture, one for each person (their activities grouped once a snapshot), placed each frame where their latest-started activity puts them at the view time, with what they carry (a can, a basket, a bucket, the weekly shop's two bags) drawn beside them (`drawItem()`), and not drawn while they're `away` at work beyond the gate (the gardener walks out to the gate at 08:30 on a working day and in from it to the kitchen at 17:30, `docs/systems/gardener.md`); each dug bed's crop is drawn each frame from its `crop` lever (`drawCrop()`: drills before the shoots, plants growing to their size in rows by crop, yellowing and drooping as the water stress coefficient falls, blackened by frost and fading back over a week for potatoes, and ripe tomatoes, radishes and bean pods showing), and the honesty box on its post by the side gate; the pests and wildlife are drawn from the sim's populations with cosmetic scatter seeded from the game's seed (`src/ui/map/life.ts`: slugs creeping out after dark and their slime, aphid clusters, bees, ladybirds, the cat; blight browning and bites out of nibbled plants, marigolds and a bed's border in `drawCrop()` and `drawLive()`), the gardener's torch lights the ground on the dusk patrol, a ring pulses round the Explain card's place, and a night layer darkens with the sun (`src/ui/map/daylight.ts`, day length at 51.5° N from the date, cosmetic). **The steady light** (`steadyLight()` beside `darkness()`): when a game day lasts under `STEADY_DAY_S` (8) real seconds at the pace it's passing, a quiet night's included (`daySeconds()`), the night layer stops following the hour and holds a steady daylight, or a gentle dim through a quiet night (`--map-night-quiet`) once it has been asked for a second (`SETTLE_S`); and whatever the speed, a load or a jump, it changes by at most `LIGHT_MOST` (0.2) opacity a second, so the full night takes about 1¾ s to fall. So 1× in the back garden still cycles day and night, and 2×, the allotment and every level above hold, far inside WCAG 2.3.1's three flashes a second; reduced motion gets the same. The top bar's clock and the moon say it's night. Any later full-map light layer (a dawn glow, lightning) keeps to the same limit. Shapes are drawn in CSS pixels through a camera that fits the level on the canvas, so they stay crisp at any size. A tap on a creature opens its Explain card (`creatureAt()`); otherwise it selects the place under it in the panel. `src/ui/MapView.tsx` mounts it, resizes it, and follows `prefers-color-scheme` and `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **The style** (`src/ui/map/draw.ts`): the owner's pick, flat, top-down and soft (`docs/specs/overgrow/art-styles.html`, style A): rounded shapes with no outlines, soft shadows, greens, soil browns and cream. Each node kind has its drawing; an undug bed plot is grass with a dashed edge. Every colour is a `--map-*` token in `src/ui/styles/tokens.css`, light and dark, read by `src/ui/map/palette.ts`.
- **The top bar** (`src/ui/TopBar.tsx`): the level, the date and hour with the air's temperature, the money, the carbon dial (a needle on a log scale with the kg beside it, so it isn't colour alone; the money and the dial open their Explain cards) and pause with 1×, 2×, 4×, 8× and 16× as pressed buttons (below 960 px inside its padding 4×, 8× and 16× share one button that cycles them, so the bar keeps its row with a December's money and dial), with a moon before them while a quiet night passes quickly (`docs/systems/clock.md`); the temperature, the money and the dial each show once they've unfolded (`docs/systems/unfolding.md`). It steps by its own width with container queries (the owner's win W2): below 600 px inside its padding the temperature goes, and below 640 px pause and the speeds fold into one button that shows the speed and cycles it, a tap on a paused game resuming (W11), so it's one row at 640 px and up and at most two below. The speed shown is the last one set, even when the map is a step behind.
- **The panel** (`src/ui/Panel.tsx`): tabs along its head, as pressed buttons (Garden, Shed and Kitchen, each shown once it has unfolded, `src/ui/ShedTab.tsx` the Shed's; Goals comes later). The Garden tab (`src/ui/GardenTab.tsx`) has the gardener's card (the job in hand, from their latest-started activity, and a meter of the day's hours left), the plan (for each dug bed its crop and status, what to sow or the rotation, and from when; and the moisture below which the gardener waters; each change a `plan` command; and for each pest the garden's crops draw, the pest policy as a `policy` command, and a border of marigolds along each bed's edge), then the garden's places as buttons and what the selected one holds (a bed's crop and quality too) (`src/ui/format.ts`, concise UK English with units): for a bed or the lawn, its moisture (the share of its water for roots, or "Full, draining"), organic matter, nitrate, phosphorus, potassium, soil health and the carbon in its soil, its slugs, aphids, blight, what pests have taken and its border, and what happened there in the last week; each number with an Explain card opens it. The Kitchen tab (`src/ui/KitchenTab.tsx`) shows the day's ask by group with a meter of what met it at the last meal, the last week's share, what's in the kitchen and the honesty box, and what's been picked, eaten, sold (and for how much) and wasted. On a portrait phone it's a sheet under the map, at most 45 % of the height, that folds to its heading; beside the map on tablets and wide screens, and beside a narrower one on a phone on its side (`src/ui/styles/page.css`): screen-width media queries choose only the sheet or the side panel, and the map, the panel and the top bar are size containers the rest steps by; the page keeps the safe areas clear on every side (`env(safe-area-inset-*)`), the top bar at the top and the card's footer at the bottom.
- **Access.** Every button is at least 40 px, every panel works by keyboard with a visible focus ring, the map's places are all in the panel's list, and the map is labelled for screen readers. The `layout` check holds the sizes and the keyboard; the `scene` check holds the drawing, the interpolation, the reduced-motion jump, the steady light (at 16× across two game days, with and without reduced motion, and the night falling at 1×), the weather (rain only while it rains and still under reduced motion, frost, the soil's colour), the Canvas 2D fallback and a repeating seeded screenshot; the `garden` check holds the crops and the gardener drawn, and the tabs working; the `explain` check holds the card, the badges and a creature's tap.

## The playable garden, round two

- **The big buys** are drawn where they work (`drawNode()` and `drawSites()` in `src/ui/map/draw.ts`): the greenhouse's glass and glazing bars over its border, the hens' run with their house and the three of them wandering it by day (a cosmetic loop from the clock) and in the house at night, the fruit cage's bushes under a net with red fruit showing as it ripens, raised beds' timber boards, and the rainwater tank against the house past the shed. The colours are tokens (`--map-timber`, `--map-tank`, `--map-run`, `--map-hen-house`, `--map-hen`, `--map-comb`, `--map-net`, `--map-berry`).
- **The small rewards** (`src/ui/juice.ts`, drawn by `src/ui/Juice.tsx` over the map like the badges): a purchase pulses where the thing now stands (the buy's `buying` effect is at its place, `placeOf()` in `src/sim/shed.ts`), a sale sends a coin up from the honesty box towards the purse, the first harvest bursts in leaves over its bed, and a sowing drops seed on its bed. Each lasts 1.6 s; under reduced motion each is a still.
