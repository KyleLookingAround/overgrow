# The map and the page's shell

The page (`src/ui/App.tsx`): a top bar (`src/ui/TopBar.tsx`), the map filling the rest (`src/ui/MapView.tsx`, drawn by `src/ui/map/renderer.ts` and `src/ui/map/draw.ts`), and the panel beside it or below it (`src/ui/Panel.tsx`, with its Garden and Kitchen tabs in `src/ui/GardenTab.tsx` and `src/ui/KitchenTab.tsx`), laid out by `src/ui/styles/page.css` with every colour and size in `src/ui/styles/tokens.css`, all drawn from what the clock loop's view shows. The UI changes the game only by sending commands. Over the map sit the badges, the Explain card and the notices (`docs/systems/explain.md`), the first plan's card and the goal bar, one card at a time (`docs/systems/unfolding.md`), and the pests and wildlife are drawn by `src/ui/map/life.ts`. The shell follows the UI rules taken from Final Call (`docs/decisions/ADR-2026-09-29-ui-from-final-call.md`).

- **The renderer** (`src/ui/map/renderer.ts`): PixiJS on WebGL, and Pixi's own Canvas 2D renderer where WebGL is missing (`docs/decisions/ADR-2026-09-28-webgl-map.md`). Its layers, bottom up: the ground (every node with a box, `drawGround()`: the hedge, the lawn, the paths, the house wall, the fence and the structures), drawn once into one Graphics and redrawn only when the graph's `rev`, a bed plot being dug (`isDug()`, which the panel shares), the size or the colours change; the live soil (`drawLive()`, each frame, interpolated between the two snapshots: each dug bed's soil paling towards `--map-bed-dry` as its surface dries and darkening towards `--map-bed-wet` when soaked, `wetness()`, and darkening at once while rain falls on it, drying over three hours after, `surfaceWet()`; the lawn darkening under the same film; puddles on the paths while it lies; the butt's water); the tilth's crumbs over the dug beds (`drawTilth()`, once, so the colour changes under them); what grows (`drawGrown()`, once a snapshot: each dug bed's crop from its `crop` lever, `drawCrop()`: drills before the shoots, two seed leaves until a quarter grown, plants growing to their size in rows in their crop's silhouette (`src/ui/map/plants.ts`: a rosette, a fan of blades, a bush, a climber on a cane, a flowering rosette or a sward), fuller with a sheen when ready, drooping and yellowing as the water stress coefficient falls or the crop runs short of its nutrients, blackened by frost and fading back over a week for potatoes, and ripe tomatoes, radishes and bean pods showing; the borders; the kit in use; the big buys; the cordons' blossom in April and berries after; and the leaves lying on the lawn from mid-October to mid-December); what's over the plants (`drawOver()`, each frame: the hens stepping about their run, and the frost's rime over the lawn and along the beds' and the shed's edges); the pests, wildlife and delights (`src/ui/map/life.ts`, from the sim's populations with cosmetic scatter seeded from the game's seed: slugs creeping out after dark and their slime, aphid clusters, bees, ladybirds, the cat, a butterfly in summer, a robin in winter, steam off a working heap on a cold morning); the people (particles from one atlas of six frames, the gardener and the household standing or stepping either foot, one for each person, their activities grouped once a snapshot, placed each frame where their latest-started activity puts them at the view time and stepping through the walk as they move (a crowd past two hundred glides in one frame, through a container that uploads positions alone), with what they carry (a can, a basket, a bucket, the weekly shop's two bags) drawn beside them (`drawItem()`), and not drawn while they're `away` at work beyond the gate (`docs/systems/gardener.md`)); the rain, a pool of particles falling while the sim says it rains and drawn as flakes falling slower while the air is at or below 1 °C (`docs/systems/weather.md`); the night layer darkening with the sun and the dawn layer glowing warm as it crosses the horizon (`src/ui/map/daylight.ts`, day length at 51.5° N from the date, cosmetic); the gardener's torch on the dusk patrol; and a ring pulsing round the Explain card's place. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the rain stops falling but stays shown, the figures stand rather than step, and every other animation takes its time from the tick's hour. Shapes are drawn in CSS pixels through a camera that fits the level on the canvas, so they stay crisp at any size. A tap on a creature opens its Explain card (`creatureAt()`); otherwise it selects the place under it in the panel. `src/ui/MapView.tsx` mounts it, resizes it, and follows `prefers-color-scheme` and `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **The style** (`docs/specs/map-art.md`): the owner's pick, flat, top-down and soft (`docs/specs/overgrow/art-styles.html`, style A): rounded shapes with no outlines, light from the top left, soft shadows down and to the right, greens, soil browns and cream. `src/ui/map/draw.ts` draws the garden with it; an undug bed plot is grass with a dashed edge. Every colour is a `--map-*` token in `src/ui/styles/tokens.css`, light and dark, read by `src/ui/map/palette.ts`.
- **The style kit** (`src/ui/map/kit.ts`, `src/ui/map/plants.ts`): the pieces every level's map draws with, each a function of a rectangle in CSS pixels, a scale in pixels per metre and the palette: the lawn with its mowing stripes and tufts, the hedge, a fence with posts and a gate's gap, dug soil with its rim, tilth, a gravel path, puddles, a house wall with its door and windows, a shed with a pitched roof, a heap, a butt, a tap, fallen leaves, a figure in three frames, a hen, a bee, a slug, a ladybird, a butterfly, a robin, steam, a cane; and the plants: a silhouette a shape with its lit top, a seedling, a sward, and the leaf colours for a plant's state. Part 7's allotment draws its plots with the same pieces at their own scale, and a sealed node's tile uses the same ground, tints and shadows. The `scene` check's screenshots and `build/map-shots.mjs` (every season, hour and weather at phone and desktop sizes, light and dark) are how a change to it is looked at.
- **The top bar** (`src/ui/TopBar.tsx`): the level, the date and hour with the air's temperature, the money, the carbon dial (a needle on a log scale with the kg beside it, so it isn't colour alone; the money and the dial open their Explain cards) and pause with 1×, 2× and 4× as pressed buttons; the temperature, the money and the dial each show once they've unfolded (`docs/systems/unfolding.md`). It steps by its own width with container queries (the owner's win W2): below 600 px inside its padding the temperature goes, and below 640 px the four speeds fold into one button that shows the speed and cycles it, a tap on a paused game resuming (W11), so it's one row at 640 px and up and at most two below. The speed shown is the last one set, even when the map is a step behind.
- **The panel** (`src/ui/Panel.tsx`): tabs along its head, as pressed buttons (Garden, Shed and Kitchen, each shown once it has unfolded, `src/ui/ShedTab.tsx` the Shed's; Goals comes later). The Garden tab (`src/ui/GardenTab.tsx`) has the gardener's card (the job in hand, from their latest-started activity, and a meter of the day's hours left), the plan (for each dug bed its crop and status, what to sow or the rotation, and from when; and the moisture below which the gardener waters; each change a `plan` command; and for each pest the garden's crops draw, the pest policy as a `policy` command, and a border of marigolds along each bed's edge), then the garden's places as buttons and what the selected one holds (a bed's crop and quality too) (`src/ui/format.ts`, concise UK English with units): for a bed or the lawn, its moisture (the share of its water for roots, or "Full, draining"), organic matter, nitrate, phosphorus, potassium, soil health and the carbon in its soil, its slugs, aphids, blight, what pests have taken and its border, and what happened there in the last week; each number with an Explain card opens it. The Kitchen tab (`src/ui/KitchenTab.tsx`) shows the day's ask by group with a meter of what met it at the last meal, the last week's share, what's in the kitchen and the honesty box, and what's been picked, eaten, sold (and for how much) and wasted. On a portrait phone it's a sheet under the map, at most 45 % of the height, that folds to its heading; beside the map on tablets and wide screens, and beside a narrower one on a phone on its side (`src/ui/styles/page.css`): screen-width media queries choose only the sheet or the side panel, and the map, the panel and the top bar are size containers the rest steps by; the page keeps the safe areas clear on every side (`env(safe-area-inset-*)`), the top bar at the top and the card's footer at the bottom.
- **Access.** Every button is at least 40 px, every panel works by keyboard with a visible focus ring, the map's places are all in the panel's list, and the map is labelled for screen readers. The `layout` check holds the sizes and the keyboard; the `scene` check holds the drawing, each crop's silhouette, the interpolation, the reduced-motion jump, the weather (rain only while it rains and still under reduced motion, frost, the soil's colour, puddles, the dawn's glow), the Canvas 2D fallback and a repeating seeded screenshot; the `garden` check holds the crops and the gardener drawn, and the tabs working; the `explain` check holds the card, the badges and a creature's tap.

## The playable garden, round two

- **The big buys** are drawn where they work (`drawNode()` and `drawSites()` in `src/ui/map/draw.ts`): the greenhouse's glass and glazing bars over its border, the hens' run with their house and the three of them wandering it by day (a cosmetic loop from the clock) and in the house at night, the fruit cage's bushes under a net with red fruit showing as it ripens, raised beds' timber boards, and the rainwater tank against the house past the shed. The colours are tokens (`--map-timber`, `--map-tank`, `--map-run`, `--map-hen-house`, `--map-hen`, `--map-comb`, `--map-net`, `--map-berry`).
- **The small rewards** (`src/ui/juice.ts`, drawn by `src/ui/Juice.tsx` over the map like the badges): a purchase pulses where the thing now stands (the buy's `buying` effect is at its place, `placeOf()` in `src/sim/shed.ts`), a sale sends a coin up from the honesty box towards the purse, the first harvest bursts in leaves over its bed, and a sowing drops seed on its bed. Each lasts 1.6 s; under reduced motion each is a still.
