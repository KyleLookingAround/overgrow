# The map and the page's shell

The page (`src/ui/App.tsx`): a top bar (`src/ui/TopBar.tsx`), the map filling the rest (`src/ui/MapView.tsx`, drawn by `src/ui/map/renderer.ts` and `src/ui/map/draw.ts`), and the panel beside it or below it (`src/ui/Panel.tsx`), laid out by `src/ui/styles/page.css` with every colour and size in `src/ui/styles/tokens.css`, all drawn from what the clock loop's view shows. The UI changes the game only by sending commands.

- **The renderer** (`src/ui/map/renderer.ts`): PixiJS on WebGL, and Pixi's own Canvas 2D renderer where WebGL is missing (`docs/decisions/ADR-2026-09-28-webgl-map.md`). The ground (every node with a box) is drawn once into one Graphics and redrawn only when the graph's `rev`, a bed plot being dug (`isDug()`, which the panel shares), the size or the colours change; the butt's water, each dug bed's soil (paling towards `--map-bed-dry` as its surface dries and darkening towards `--map-bed-wet` when soaked, `wetness()`, and darkening at once while rain falls on it, drying over three hours after, `surfaceWet()`) and the frost's rime are redrawn each frame, interpolated between the two snapshots, and rain falls as a pool of particles while the sim says it rains (`docs/systems/weather.md`); under `prefers-reduced-motion` the rain stops falling but stays shown; people are particles from one texture, placed each frame from the snapshot's activities at the view time; a night layer darkens with the sun (`src/ui/map/daylight.ts`, day length at 51.5° N from the date, cosmetic). Shapes are drawn in CSS pixels through a camera that fits the level on the canvas, so they stay crisp at any size. A tap selects the place under it in the panel. `src/ui/MapView.tsx` mounts it, resizes it, and follows `prefers-color-scheme` and `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **The style** (`src/ui/map/draw.ts`): the owner's pick, flat, top-down and soft (`docs/specs/overgrow/art-styles.html`, style A): rounded shapes with no outlines, soft shadows, greens, soil browns and cream. Each node kind has its drawing; an undug bed plot is grass with a dashed edge. Every colour is a `--map-*` token in `src/ui/styles/tokens.css`, light and dark, read by `src/ui/map/palette.ts`.
- **The top bar** (`src/ui/TopBar.tsx`): the level, the date and hour with the air's temperature (hidden below 480 px wide, where it would push the speeds onto a third row), the money, the carbon dial (a needle on a log scale with the kg beside it, so it isn't colour alone) and pause with 1×, 2× and 4× as pressed buttons. The speed shown is the last one set, even when the map is a step behind.
- **The panel** (`src/ui/Panel.tsx`): the garden's places as buttons and what the selected one holds (`src/ui/format.ts`, concise UK English with units): for a bed or the lawn, its moisture (the share of its water for roots, or "Full, draining"), organic matter, nitrate, phosphorus, potassium, soil health and the carbon in its soil. On a portrait phone it's a sheet under the map, at most 45 % of the height, that folds to its heading; beside the map on tablets and wide screens, and beside a narrower one on a phone on its side (`src/ui/styles/page.css`). The garden's tabs (Garden, Shed, Kitchen, Goals) replace the list as parts 3 and 6 fill them, each shown once it has something in it.
- **Access.** Every button is at least 40 px, every panel works by keyboard with a visible focus ring, the map's places are all in the panel's list, and the map is labelled for screen readers. The `layout` check holds the sizes and the keyboard; the `scene` check holds the drawing, the interpolation, the reduced-motion jump, the weather (rain only while it rains and still under reduced motion, frost, the soil's colour), the Canvas 2D fallback and a repeating seeded screenshot.
