# The carry-over rule (sealing, events across scales and the step-up test)

The pure maths of the founding spec's carry-over rule, written ahead of part 7 (`src/sim/ladder.ts`, with its tests in `src/sim/ladder.test.ts` and its numbers in `src/data/ladder-rules.ts`; the ladder's clock is a different file, `src/data/ladder.ts`, see `docs/systems/clock.md`). Nothing calls it yet: part 7 wires it. It is a rule of the game, not a real-world model, so it has no sources; its header says which spec section each function implements.

- **The history a level keeps** (`History`, `emptyHistory(level)`, `record()`): a ring of `Sample`s capped at the level's window, which is the last full cycle of its rhythm (`RHYTHMS`; the owner's answers on #29, Q15: each level lasts long enough for its slowest lever to pay back once, so the garden's and the allotment's window is the last full **year**, not the 28 days and the season the spec first had; the smallholding and above are a year too and the planet's a decade, all proposals for the bot's baselines to set). **The garden and the allotment sample weekly, not daily**: a daily year is 365 samples (about 75 kB of saved JSON with the mix, demand and hours in each) where a weekly one is 53 (about 11 kB), and the year's shape, which is what Reliability and the step-up read, doesn't need the days. The smallholding to the town sample daily (365), levels 6 and 7 weekly and the planet monthly. A sample is the period's food delivered (kg), its quality (0–100), what running cost (£), emissions less sinks (kg CO₂e), optionally freshness (days), the slow stocks behind Health (soil, water, kit, goodwill, herd and wildlife, each 0–100, the ones the level has), and, when the level has them, the output by product group, the demand and the hours (below). `record()` returns a new ring, so the level keeps it as a lever that's replaced, never changed in place. It is JSON, never the level's detail.
- **Totals over a window** (`windowTotals()`): the five headline numbers, the carbon and the land, as the graph's `Totals`. Output, Upkeep and carbon are per-day means; Quality and Freshness are weighted by output; Reliability is `reliability()` of the per-day output, 100 × (1 − the coefficient of variation), clamped 0–100 (0 for a series with no mean), and restated per day for a level that samples weekly or monthly (its spread scaled up by the square root of the sample's days, so `sealedTick` can scale it back down for any tick); Health is `healthIndex()` of the last sample (weights soil 0.35, water 0.2, kit, goodwill and herd 0.15, wildlife 0.1, shared among the parts a level has), because it is a stock, not a flow; land is as it stands (m², as `Totals` carries it). `full` says the whole window is in the ring.
- **A sealed node** (`SealedNode`: totals, the plan, its events, the game hour it was last ticked; `sealNode()` makes one from a level's totals). `sealedTick(node, hours, rng)` moves it on to a game hour: Output × the events' share × Health's factor × (1 + noise). The noise is lognormal with mean 1 and a spread of (100 − Reliability) % a day (a tick of several days averages down by the square root of them, so a week isn't as lumpy as a day, and the spread stays the series' per-day one); it never goes negative, whatever the node, and always takes two draws so the stream doesn't depend on the node. `healthFactor()` is 1 % of Output for each point of Health below 50. Health drifts toward `plan.health` by at most a point a season, which is 1 ÷ 91.25 a day. Upkeep is paid, and the emissions and land follow `plan.carbon` and `plan.land` where the plan sets them. Health's penalty applies to the Output as sealed: the step-up needs Health of 50 or more, so a node sealed through the offer has none to double-count, but one sealed below 50 by another route would lose the penalty twice. It returns the node after the tick, the kg delivered and lost to events, the £ paid and the kg CO₂e emitted (negative is a sink).
- **Events across scales** (`GameEvent`, `showEvent()`, `eventKgLost()`, `eventFactor()`): an event has a home level, a size (the share of a node's output it destroys while it lasts) and a duration in days; it loses `size × Output × days` kg wherever it is shown. `showEvent(event, level, {node, region})` gives what a level draws, or null below the home level: `thing` at home, `tile` one level up (`text`: "Output −20 % for 10 days"), `region` two or more up (`tint` the event's kind, `text`: "pest year in the east: veg −5 %", the size being the node's share of the region's output, so the kg still match). A duration shorter than one tick of the showing level (`tickDays(level)`: an hour at levels 1 and 2, a day at 3 to 5, a week at 6 and 7) rounds up to a tick with the size scaled down, so the kg are the same. Events on one node multiply (two events of size 0.5 leave a quarter, not nothing), so each event's kg are exact alone and slightly less added together. `eventKgLost()` takes the output of what the event is shown on: the region's for a region. A sealed node applies an event by the hours of it inside each tick (`eventFactor()`), so any tick length loses the same kg. Places and labels are invented ones.
- **Inflating** (`inflateTarget()`, `carryCheck()`, `layoutKey()`): the target is the totals as sealed, over the level's window, to within 5 % (`INFLATE_TOLERANCE`); `carryCheck(sealed, rebuilt)` says whether a rebuilt level's first-cycle totals are inside it and names the numbers that aren't (each against the sealed value, or against a floor per number so a node with almost no carbon isn't held to 5 % of almost nothing). It is the `carry` test's core. `layoutKey(seed, nodeId)` is the seed of the rebuilt level's layout, from the game's seed and the node's id alone, so the same node always looks the same (`layoutRng()` makes the dice).
- **The step-up offer** (`stepUpStatus()`, `STEP_UP`): Output at least 1.5 kg a day, Reliability at least 60 and Health at least 50 over a full window (the level's last full cycle: a year at the garden, Q15; it was 28 days), proposed by the spec and held as data for part 4's baselines to set. It returns the three requirements furthest-from-target first (by shortfall as a share of the target), each with its value, target, progress for the bar and a one-line hint, the `binding` one (null when all are met, whether or not the cycle is full), and `ready`, which also waits for the window to fill (win W18: the goal bar names what's holding the player back).
- **What a level carries beside the five numbers** (`LadderTotals`, which extends the graph's `Totals`, so `graph.ts` is unchanged and a node's `totals` takes it as it is; part 7 may hoist the fields into `Totals`). Three optional fields, so a level that has none carries none. **Output by product group** (`outputByGroup`, kg a day; `PRODUCT_GROUPS`: the kitchen's potatoes, salads, tomatoes and greens, and room for eggs, meat, milk and grain): `sampleOf(groups, rest)` makes a sample whose `output` is the sum of its groups, so the headline and the mix can't disagree; the window takes their per-day means, and a sealed tick keeps the mix: `SealedTick.byGroup` and `lostByGroup` split the delivered and the lost kg in the node's own proportions and add up to `output` and `lost`. **Demand** (`demand`: kg a day by group, and £ a day spent) and **hours** (`hours`: people-hours a day had and used) ride in the sample beside them and come out per day, weekly samples spread over their seven days. `carryCheck` compares the mix group by group when the sealed totals have one, so a rebuilt level must reproduce it.
- **Wildlife** (`wildlifeIndex({flowers, hedges, margins})`, weights 0.4, 0.4 and 0.2, rough): the parts a level has, as one 0–100 index, which the sample records as `health.wildlife`. It is a sixth part of Health with weight 0.1; a level that doesn't record it is unchanged.
- **A parent from its sealed children** (`sumTotals(children)`, needed from level 3 up: a field of plots, a town of households, a region of farms): Output, demand (by group and £), hours, upkeep, carbon and land add; Quality, Freshness and Health are weighted by output (a plain mean of Health when nothing is delivered); the groups still add up to Output when the children's do. Reliability is that of the **summed series** when every child brings its per-day series over the same window (two children swinging against each other are steady together), and otherwise comes from the children's spreads taken as independent: their variances add, so `n` equal children are steadier together by `√n`. An empty parent is empty totals.
- **An event's money loss** (`eventValueLost(shown, outputKgPerDay, pricePerKg)`, and `SealedTick.lostGBP` from `sealedTick`'s optional `price` argument, a flat £ a kg or a price by group; a price by group needs the node's mix, so a node sealed without one reports 0 for it: give it a flat price): the kg lost times the price, the same at every level, for the insurance to pay out on.
- **Speed** (Node 22 with `vite-node`, the session's 4-core 2.1 GHz Xeon, after a warm-up; re-measured with the year's window, the mix, demand and hours and the summing function): a sealed tick with a mix is about 0.7 µs (0.2 µs without one), so 5,000 sealed nodes (the nation's most) cost about 3 ms a tick including their new objects; `record()` about 0.8 µs; `windowTotals()` about 24 µs for a garden's 53 weekly samples and 150 µs for a daily year of 365 samples, each carrying a mix, demand and hours (it was 0.7 µs for 28 samples and 4 µs for 365 without them), so the goal bar can still read it every day for nothing; `sumTotals()` about 2 µs for twelve plots, 14 µs for a hundred nodes and 13 µs for twelve with a 53-sample series each. `stepUpStatus()`, `windowTotals()` and `record()` are in `dist/` since part 6a's goal bar; the rest isn't until it's wired.

**Approved on #29 (29 Sep 2026), not yet in the code:** the owner approved Q2 (a sealed node carries demand, kg a day by product group and £ a day, and hours, people-hours a day had and used), Q3 (Output by product group) and Q6 (a wildlife part in Health), and Q15 (the garden's window becomes the last full year, not 28 days); a models-ahead session will add them to `src/sim/ladder.ts`.

## Wiring

For part 7 (sealing and the step up), and part 9 for the first zoom back in. The brief was new files only, so none of this is done:

- **The history.** Part 6a wired the garden's first ring for the goal bar (`src/sim/goal.ts`: the kitchen's `goal` lever, a weekly sample from the first Monday of output eaten and sold, carbon, and soil health; `docs/systems/unfolding.md`); the rest of this is for the sealing part. The garden keeps a `History` for level 1 and calls `record()` once a sample (a week at the garden and the allotment, a day from level 3, `History.sampleDays`) from a `day` system with that period's `Sample`, summed over its days (`sampleOf()` when the harvest is by group, plus the household's demand and the people's hours if it has them): harvest and honesty-box sales taken to the kitchen or sold, the kitchen's mean quality, the gardener's and tools' upkeep, the net of the heap, the crops and the soil in kg CO₂e, and the slow stocks (`health`: soil from the beds' health index, water from the butt and the soil's moisture, kit from the tools, goodwill and herd where the level has them). Set `node.totals` from `windowTotals()` each day so the goal bar and the step-up card read the graph.
- **The offer.** The goal bar shows `stepUpStatus(windowTotals(history))`: `requirements` in order, `binding` first, with its `hint`. Latch the offer once `ready` so progress that falls back doesn't take it away. The step-up card lists what it opens from the same tables the game gates on (win W4).
- **Sealing.** On the step up, `sealNode(windowTotals(history).totals, ctx.hours)` becomes a lever on the allotment's node under `SEALED.lever` (`'sealed'`), the history moving into `History`'s place for the level above. Set `plan.health` (and `carbon`, `land`) when the player changes the node's plan. Give the node a `food` stock (product `produce`) and a `money` stock (upkeep is skipped without one) and list `sealedSystem` in `src/sim/systems.ts`, after the systems that change the node's plan. It moves `harvest` from `growth` into the node's `food`, `upkeep` from its `money` to `bought`, emissions from `bought` into the `atmosphere` node's carbon and sinks from it into the node's carbon; part 7 may prefer other boundaries. `sealedSystem` runs on the `day` tick and works out the days since the last tick from the game hour, so it copes with a level whose step is longer than a day. No change to `graph.ts` is needed: `Totals` already has every number.
- **Events.** Part 5's pests (and the weather) create `GameEvent`s with a home level; add them to a sealed node's `events` (the tick prunes what's over). The tile draws `showEvent(e, level).text` with the event's icon by `kind`; a regional tint draws by `tint`.
- **Inflating and the `carry` check.** Rebuild the level below from `inflateTarget()`, seed its layout with `layoutRng(seed, nodeId)`, run its first cycle into a fresh `History`, and assert `carryCheck(target.totals, windowTotals(history).totals).ok`. The garden's own step-up is the first: a sealed garden's Output must match its last full year within 1 %, which is `windowTotals()` itself.
- **Save.** `History` and `SealedNode` are plain JSON; `SealedNode.totals.land` and `plan` are records, so the save takes them as they are.
