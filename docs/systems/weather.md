# The weather

The level's weather, drawn each day by a Richardson-type generator (`src/sim/models/weather.ts`) from the station's monthly normals (`src/data/climate-normals.ts`), with its plausibility test in `src/sim/models/weather.test.ts`; the map draws it (`src/ui/map/draw.ts`, `src/ui/map/renderer.ts`) and the top bar shows the temperature (`src/ui/TopBar.tsx`).

- **The station.** An invented lowland station in southern England at 51.5° N, 60 m up, whose monthly maximum and minimum, rainfall, rain days (1 mm or more), sunshine and air frosts sit inside the Met Office's 1991–2020 normals for such places (Open Government Licence). No real place is named.
- **Each day** (`nextDay()`), drawn on the first hour step of the day from the weather system's own dice (`systemRng()`), so adding it changed no other system's draws: wet or dry by a first-order Markov chain whose long-run share of wet days is the month's, with a wet day more likely after a wet day (by 0.3); a wet day's rain as 1 mm plus a gamma draw (shape 0.75) with the month's mean; one spell of rain a day, its start and length drawn; the maximum and minimum from the normals interpolated between mid-months, shifted on wet days (cooler afternoons, warmer nights) and on dry days the other way so the month's mean holds, plus correlated residuals that persist from yesterday's (0.65 and 0.6); sunshine hours from the month's share of the day, less on wet days, with its own persistence. The day's extraterrestrial radiation and length come from FAO-56 (`sunOn()`).
- **Where it's kept.** The day is the `weather` lever on the level's air node (`atmosphere`): saved with the graph, sent to the page with the snapshot (as a new object once a day, so the deltas carry it only then), and read by the water and soil models (`weatherOf()`). No command can set it: the weather system refuses a `plan`, `policy` or `law` on it. A new game has it `null` until the first step. The crops read it too, for degree days and the hour's frost.
- **The hours.** `hourOf(day, t)` shapes an hour of the day from the day: the temperature rising from the minimum at sunrise to the maximum at 15:00 and falling back through the night; the grass up to 4 °C colder than the air on a clear night (less when cloudy), and frost while it's below 0 °C; rain falling evenly through the day's spell. `rainBetween()` and `lightBetween()` give the rain and the share of the day's sunlight between two hours, for the water model. The map and the top bar call the same pure functions at the view time, so what's drawn is the sim's weather, never announced as text.
- **The warming index** (`warmingIndex()`): °C above the 1991–2020 baseline, the carbon in the level's air node (kg CO₂e) times IPCC AR6's TCRE (0.45 °C per 1,000 Gt CO₂), read each day. At the garden a few kilograms come to nothing. From part 7, sealing carries a level's carbon up the ladder, so the nation's and the planet's air nodes hold enough for it to show: at the planet the air starts with the world's emissions since the baseline (about 2,400 Gt CO₂ gives about 1 °C). By season (`PER_DEGREE` in `src/data/climate-normals.ts`), each degree warms the means (0.9 °C in winter to 1.3 °C in summer), adds 6 % to winter rain and takes 10 % from summer's, makes each wet day's rain 7 % heavier (fewer wet days make up the rest), and widens summer's spread of maximums, so hot days come faster than the mean alone would bring them. The test holds a warming of 2 to warmer means, wetter winters, drier summers and more hot and heavy-rain days.
- **The shower and the rime** (`src/ui/map/renderer.ts`, `src/ui/map/draw.ts`, cosmetic): while the day's spell is falling, streaks cross the garden, as many as the rain is heavy, the shower's front coming in from the west over 0.3 game hours and leaving to the east; under `prefers-reduced-motion` they stop falling but stay shown, moving only per tick. While the grass is below 0 °C a pale rime (`--map-frost`, up to `--map-frost-max`) lies over the garden, whiter the harder the frost. The renderer's `stats().weather` gives the drops, the frost and the soil drawn, for the `scene` check.
- **Levels with longer steps.** At a day's step the generator draws one day a step. At a week's or a month's it draws each day of the step in turn, persisting from one to the next, and keeps them in order as the last day's `step`, so the water and soil models go through the step day by day (`src/sim/models/water.test.ts` holds a day's, a week's and a month's step to the hourly balance). Pests (part 5) read `weatherOf()` for the Smith period and slugs after rain.
- **Explain.** A frost is noted as an effect on the lawn (by the crop model's hour, `docs/systems/explain.md`), rain as a flow at every place it falls; the map's frost badge and the rain open their cards.

## The playable garden, round two

- **The forecast** (the air's `forecast` lever, `forecastOf()`): at each hourly morning's draw the weather also draws tomorrow from the dice it will be drawn with (the clock's `rngAt`), so the forecast comes true (to the warming's last decimal place), and counts the days in a row with less than a millimetre of rain (`dry`). `tonight()` is the coldest the grass gets from this evening to tomorrow's mid-morning. The frost and dry-spell cards read them (`src/ui/decisions.ts`). A garden's hourly steps only.
