# The gardener

The person who does everything the plan decides (`src/sim/gardener.ts`, with its test in `src/sim/gardener.test.ts`), with the hours, walking pace and job times by tool in `src/data/jobs.ts`; each job's flows go through the crop, kitchen and carbon models (`src/sim/models/crops.ts`, `src/sim/models/kitchen.ts`, `src/sim/models/carbon.ts`); the map draws them from their activities (`src/ui/map/renderer.ts`) and their card is in the Garden tab (`src/ui/GardenTab.tsx`).

- **The node.** The gardener is the `gardener` node (kind `person`, not drawn as a place): its `hours` stock is the hours left today, its levers `waterBelow` (the plan's watering line, 0–1 of the soil's available water, a half by default), `tools` (what they have: can, hands, spade, basket, bucket) and `day` (today's plan of steps, the gardener's own). The gardener system refuses a plan on `tools` or `day`, and a `waterBelow` outside 0–1.
- **Hours.** Four hours on a weekday and six at the weekend (the owner's four, #2), given each morning from the `time` boundary with yesterday's unused hours handed back; each step's time goes back to `time` when it ends, so the card's hours tick down job by job.
- **The day.** At the first step from 06:00 they plan the day in order, each job added only if all of it fits in the hours left (what doesn't waits until tomorrow): pick what's ripe (≥ 20 g) and carry it to the kitchen a basket (5 kg) at a time; water each growing bed below the line back to field capacity, a trip a can (10 L), from the butt while it has water and then the tap; for each dug, empty bed whose plan has something in season, clear its waste to the heap, spread compost from the heap (up to 5 kg/m², a bucket of 10 kg a trip), fetch seed from the shed, sow or plant it, and water it in (3 L/m²); carry the kitchen's surplus to the honesty box and its scraps (≥ 1 kg) to the heap, and waste lying on other beds; dig a plot the plan wants dug, a square metre at a time; then home to the shed to rest until tomorrow. Times: walking at 0.8 m/s between places' centres, filling a can at the butt at 7 L/min and the tap at 12, pouring a can in a minute, sowing ten minutes a m², planting twenty, clearing six, digging an hour, picking by the crop.
- **Tools** (`TOOLS`, keyed by tool): each job takes the fastest tool the gardener has for it, as hours a unit (`per`), units a trip (`trip`), hours to load at the source (`load`) and once a job (`setup`); a job without `trip` goes in one trip. Part 6's hose, drip lines and wheelbarrow add rows here and to `tools`, and nothing in the gardener's file changes.
- **Steps and activities.** Each step is an activity (`src/sim/activity.ts`): `walk`, `fetch`, `fill`, `carry`, `water`, `sow`, `plant`, `pick`, `clear`, `load`, `spread`, `dig` or `rest`, with what's carried (the can's litres, a basket's kg of a product, a bucket of compost or waste). A step is shown in the tick it starts and its effect moves in the tick it ends: water from the butt or `mains` to the bed, the crop sown, food from the bed to the kitchen (counted in the kitchen's ledger), waste to the heap, compost to the bed, land from grass to crops, food from the kitchen to the box. Every step has a new id (`g<day>-<n>`); none is ever changed once started.
- **A change of plan.** When the plan (the watering line, or any bed's `sow`, `sowFrom` or `dig`) changes during the working day (06:00–20:00), the steps already under way are kept and the rest of the day is planned again from where they'll be, with the hours left.
- **On the map** each person is drawn once, where their latest-started activity puts them, with what they carry beside them (`src/ui/map/draw.ts`, `drawItem()`); the card shows the job in hand and the hours left.
