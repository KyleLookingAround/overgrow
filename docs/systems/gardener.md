# The gardener

The person who does everything the plan decides (`src/sim/gardener.ts`, with its test in `src/sim/gardener.test.ts`), with the working day, walking pace and job times by tool in `src/data/jobs.ts` and their hours and job from the household (`src/sim/models/household.ts`, `docs/systems/household.md`); each job's flows go through the crop, kitchen and carbon models (`src/sim/models/crops.ts`, `src/sim/models/kitchen.ts`, `src/sim/models/carbon.ts`); the pest policy's work goes through the pest and biodiversity models (`src/sim/models/pests.ts`, `src/sim/models/biodiversity.ts`, with their numbers in `src/data/pests.ts` and `src/data/flowers.ts`); the map draws them from their activities (`src/ui/map/renderer.ts`) and their card is in the Garden tab (`src/ui/GardenTab.tsx`).

- **The node.** The gardener is the `gardener` node (kind `person`, not drawn as a place): its `hours` stock is the hours left today, its levers `waterBelow` (the plan's watering line, 0–1 of the soil's available water, a half by default), the pest policy's `slugs`, `aphids` and `blight` (set by `policy` commands, `docs/systems/pests.md`), `tools` (what they have: can, hands, spade, basket, bucket, and the hose and fork once bought) and `day` (today's plan of steps, the gardener's own). The gardener system refuses a plan on `tools` or `day`, and a `waterBelow` outside 0–1.
- **Hours.** The household's garden hours that weekday (`hoursOn`: `householdGardenHours`, to the minute): four on a weekday around a full-time job and six at the weekend (the owner's four, #2, now what the job leaves), given each morning from the `time` boundary with yesterday's unused hours handed back; each step's time goes back to `time` when it ends, so the card's hours tick down job by job.
- **The job.** On a day the gardener works (`commute` in the household model: out at 08:30, home at 17:30), a job that would run past the hour they leave waits: they go back to the shed, rest, walk to the gate (`leave`), are `away` until the hour they're back, and walk in from the gate to the kitchen (`home`); then the rest of the day's list. None of it takes the garden's hours. On their last working day (Friday) they come home with the weekly shop's bags, and the `home` step's end does the week's shop, pay and the rest of life (`weeklyShop`); from the second day each homecoming is noted as `commute` (the Explain card and the job's unfold key, noted with the kitchen's first ask so the first minute gets no sign of its own). A re-plan while they're away keeps the whole trip and plans from their return.
- **The day.** At the first step from 06:00 they plan the day in order, each job added only if all of it fits in the hours left (what doesn't waits until tomorrow): pick what's ripe (20 g or more; less is left, and a once-crop with less left is done) and carry it to the kitchen a basket (5 kg) at a time; water each growing bed below the line back to field capacity, a trip a can (10 L), from the butt while it has water and then the tap; for each dug, empty bed whose plan has something in season, clear its waste to the heap, spread compost from the heap (up to 5 kg/m², a bucket of 10 kg a trip), fetch seed from the shed, sow or plant it, and water it in (3 L/m²); carry the kitchen's surplus to the honesty box and its scraps (≥ 1 kg) to the heap, and waste lying on other beds; dig a plot the plan wants dug, a quarter of a square metre at a time (one bed at a time; round two); then home to the shed to rest until tomorrow. The pest policy's daytime jobs come after the watering (traps emptied, pellets scattered, aphids squashed or sprayed, blighted leaves picked off, the potatoes sprayed; a treatment only while the purse has its price), and a border of flowers is planted after the sowing where a bed's `edge` asks for one in its season. On a damp evening with the policy picking slugs, the patrol's time (10 minutes a planted bed with slugs, and the walk) is kept back from the day's hours before anything else, and after resting at the shed they go out with a torch half an hour after sunset (not before 20:00) from bed to bed. Times: walking at 0.8 m/s between places' centres, filling a can at the butt at 7 L/min and the tap at 12, pouring a can in a minute, sowing ten minutes a m², planting twenty, clearing six, digging seven hours a m² (round two; it was an hour), or 0.8 of that with the shed's digging fork (round three), picking by the crop.
- **Tools** (`TOOLS`, keyed by tool): each job takes the fastest tool the gardener has for it, as hours a unit (`per`), units a trip (`trip`), hours to load at the source (`load`) and once a job (`setup`); a job without `trip` goes in one trip. Part 6's hose, drip lines and wheelbarrow add rows here and to `tools`, and nothing in the gardener's file changes.
- **Steps and activities.** Each step is an activity (`src/sim/activity.ts`): `walk`, `fetch`, `fill`, `carry`, `water`, `sow`, `plant`, `pick`, `clear`, `load`, `spread`, `dig`, `torch` (the dusk patrol), `trap`, `pellets`, `squash`, `spray`, `deleaf` `leave`, `away`, `home` or `rest`, with what's carried (the can's litres, a basket's kg of a product, a bucket of compost or waste, the shop's bags). A step is shown in the tick it starts and its effect moves in the tick it ends: water from the butt or `mains` to the bed, the crop sown, food from the bed to the kitchen (counted in the kitchen's ledger, its quality mixed into the kitchen's), waste to the heap, compost to the bed, land from grass to crops, food from the kitchen to the box, the pest policy's work (`control()`), a border planted. Every step has a new id (`g<day>-<n>`); none is ever changed once started.
- **A change of plan.** When the plan (the watering line, the pest policy, or any bed's `sow`, `sowFrom`, `dig` or `edge`) changes during the working day (06:00–20:00), every job already under way is finished (all its steps, so a basket being picked still reaches the kitchen) and the rest of the day is planned again from where they'll be, with the hours left.
- **On the map** each person is drawn once, where their latest-started activity puts them, with what they carry beside them (`src/ui/map/draw.ts`, `drawItem()`), and not at all while `away`; the card shows the job in hand, the hours left and, once it has unfolded, their job's days and hours.

## The playable garden (#54)

- **The hose** is a tool (`TOOLS.hose`): when it's the fastest the gardener has for watering, a bed is watered in one job from the tap (`unreel`, `hose`, `water`, `reel`), sowing's watering-in too.
- **Digging** a plot takes the purse's edging and compost (`digCost`, `DIG` in `src/data/garden.ts`) as well as the spade's time: a quarter of a square metre isn't dug when the purse can't pay for it.
- **A green manure** is dug in where it stands (`dig in`, 0.06 of digging turf, `DIG_IN`, about 25 minutes a m²) when the summer plan's next crop is due; the bed is sown the next day.
- **Two notes** from each new day's plan: `long watering` (more than 45 minutes by can, `LONG_WATERING`) and `butt dry` (the can filled at the tap with the butt empty), which unfold the hose and the second butt.
- **Sowing across the beds:** each bed's planned crop is chosen knowing what the other beds grow and what's going in today (`src/sim/models/crops.ts`'s `summerCrop()`).

## The playable garden, round two

- **Seed** comes out of the purse at each sowing (`seedCost` and `paySeed` in `src/sim/shed.ts`, each crop's `seed` in `src/data/crops.ts`): a sowing waits in the day's plan until the purse has its price, and so does a border of flowers.
- **Digging is a project:** seven hours a m² with the spade (lifting the turf, digging out roots and stones, forking in compost), about 21 hours for a 3 m² bed, most of a week's spare hours, and £37 of edging and bagged compost. The gardener digs one bed at a time, the first the plan wants dug, a quarter of a square metre a job.
- **The hens** (once bought): first thing each day a `hens` job (10 minutes, and 30 more on a Saturday for the clean-out) tops up their feed to a week's, bought from the purse as it's used (`hen feed`), fills the trough from the tap, carries the eggs to the kitchen (`collecting eggs`, counted as picked in the kitchen's ledger) and on a Saturday clears the droppings to the heap (`clearOut`, `src/sim/models/livestock.ts`).
- **Soft fruit** (once the cage is bought): ripe fruit is picked at two kilos an hour and carried in a basket at a time (`pickFruit`).
