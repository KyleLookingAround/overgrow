# Biodiversity

The flowers in the garden and the wildlife they bring (`src/sim/models/biodiversity.ts`, with its plausibility test in `src/sim/models/biodiversity.test.ts`), from the numbers in `src/data/flowers.ts` and the marigolds in `src/data/crops.ts`; the crops take the pollinators' visits (`src/sim/models/crops.ts`) and the pests the ladybirds (`src/sim/models/pests.ts`); the gardener plants a border (`src/sim/gardener.ts`), the Garden tab plans it (`src/ui/GardenTab.tsx`) and the map draws the bees, ladybirds, flowers and the cat (`src/ui/map/life.ts`, `src/ui/map/draw.ts`).

- **Flowers.** French marigolds are a crop the plan can sow in a bed (`marigolds`, planted out from 20 May to 30 June, in flower from 250 degree days above 8 °C for 1,100 more, killed by frost, yielding no food, never in the rotation: `flower: true`), or a border along a bed's edge: the bed's `edge` lever (a `plan`, `'none'` or a flower), which the gardener plants in its season in 12 minutes as the bed's `border` lever (`Border`: the flower, when planted, degree days since), 0.6 m² that costs the bed nothing. A border grows by degree days, flowers, and is gone after a frost (the grass at dawn below 0 °C) or once it's over. `bloom()` is the m² in flower; 1.5 m² counts in full.
- **Wildlife** (the lawn's `wildlife` lever, daily): bees about at midday follow a target of 5 (from the gardens around) plus 15 with the garden's flowers in full, from March to October once the day's mean is 10 °C, less on a wet day; their visits are bees ÷ 15, up to every flower. Ladybirds follow 2 plus 20 with flowers in full, from a mean of 10 °C, more with aphids to eat. A spray's knock (the insecticide takes bees to 60 % and ladybirds to 30 %, the fungicide ladybirds to 90 %) wears off a tenth of the way a day. The cat is out on a dry day and does nothing to the game. The biodiversity system refuses any command on `wildlife` or `border`.
- **Pollination** (`pollination()`): a pollinated crop's yield is 1 − D × (1 − visits), with Klein et al.'s dependence D, 5 % for French beans and tomatoes ("little"); the crop model applies it to what ripens and notes the kilograms the bees added. The test holds a bean crop's gain with flowers at 1.5–10 %.
- **On the map**: marigolds in bloom and a border of orange along a bed's front edge, bees circling the flowers by day (or the crops, with none), ladybirds on the beds with aphids or flowers, and the cat strolling the lawn; each opens its Explain card when tapped.
