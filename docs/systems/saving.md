# Saving

The game saves on the device and carries on exactly where it was, the random stream included: the format (and, from the first release, its migrations) in `src/sim/save.ts`, the generator in `src/sim/random.ts`, the device's side in `src/app/storage.ts`, and when it saves and loads in `src/app/main.tsx`.

- **The format** (`src/sim/save.ts`): versioned JSON (`SAVE_VERSION`, 11 today: part 7's step up added the goal's latch `offered`, the allotment's graph at level 2 and the garden kept below in `ladder` (`docs/systems/allotment.md`); before that 9: round two of the playable garden added the state's `answered`, the beds' `raised`, `fallow`, `fleece` and `warmed`, the gardener's `mulch` and `spell`, the kitchen's `glut` and its ledger's preserves, gifts and gluts, the air's `forecast`, the kit's `seeds`, `fleece` and `chitted`, and the big buys' nodes once bought; before that 8: part 6a renamed the unfold keys and added the goal's ring, the garden's nutrients added the nutrients a crop and its residue hold, the heap's phosphorus and potassium, and the garden's richer soils, part 6b the household node, the kitchen's cupboard and the produce's quality, and the playable garden the shed's kit, each bed's winter line and cover, and the goal's weeks fed) of the whole state less what's runtime only: the seed, the main stream's state (`Rng.state()`, `src/sim/random.ts`: saved and restored exactly, though no system draws from it yet, since each draws its own dice from the seed and the hour), the hours, level and speed, the household, the graph (with the plan, each bed's crop, the gardener's day, the kitchen's ledger, the produce's quality at the kitchen and the box, the goal's ring, and the household's members and ledger as levers on their nodes), the last tick's flows (not its effects, which are runtime only), the activities, the sealed levels (the ladder), upgrades, laws, goals, settings and what's been seen. `fromSave()` checks what it reads and throws a `SaveError` naming what's wrong; a `load` command that fails leaves the game as it was.
- **Versions and migrations.** Until the first release (part 15) saves carry no compatibility promise (`docs/decisions/ADR-2026-09-29-no-save-compatibility-before-release.md`): a change to the saved shape raises `SAVE_VERSION`, `MIGRATIONS` stays empty, and a save from an earlier build is refused ("from an earlier build"), so the page starts a new game. Part 3 raised it to 3 and dropped part 2's step; part 5 raised it to 4 (the pests, the wildlife and the pest policy), part 6a to 5, the garden's nutrients (Bug #47) to 6, and part 6b to 7. From the first release on, `MIGRATIONS[n]` turns a version-n save into version n+1, `migrate()` runs the chain one step at a time, and a saved field is never renamed or removed. A save from a newer version is always refused. `src/sim/save.test.ts` proves a save round-trips and plays on identically (the plan, the crops, the gardener's day and the kitchen included), that the chain runs, and that an earlier build's save is refused; `src/sim/index.test.ts` saves and loads in the middle of a two-year run. The save fixtures and the `migrate` check come with the first release.
- **Where it's kept** (`src/app/storage.ts`): `localStorage['overgrow-save-v1']`, the one key, every call guarded so a full or blocked store never stops the game. A move to IndexedDB once a save passes 1 MB changes only that file.
- **When** (`src/app/main.tsx`): after a new game starts, every game day, after any command that isn't a tick, when the page is hidden, and on leaving (the last save text, written at once since the page can't wait for the worker; if the page closes before the save started on hiding comes back, up to a game day's play since the last one is lost). On opening, a save is loaded; a save from a newer version of the game is left alone and the session doesn't save over it, and any other unreadable save is replaced by a new game.
