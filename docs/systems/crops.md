# Crops

What grows in each dug bed, from sowing to the compost heap (`src/sim/models/crops.ts`, with its plausibility test in `src/sim/models/crops.test.ts`), from the six crops and the marigolds in `src/data/crops.ts`; the water model takes each crop's coefficient (`src/sim/models/water.ts`), the map draws the crops (`src/ui/map/draw.ts`) and the Garden tab shows and sets the plan (`src/ui/GardenTab.tsx`).

- **The crops** (`CROPS`): salad leaves and radishes (brassicas), lettuce (daisy family), French beans (legume), potatoes and tomatoes (solanum). Each has an RHS sowing season (`sow`), sown as seed or planted out (seed potatoes, young tomatoes raised on a windowsill, not modelled), a base temperature, degree days to emergence, to the first harvest and for how long it's picked, 'once' (lettuce, radishes, potatoes: all ready together) or 'repeat' (salad leaves cut and come again, beans, tomatoes: ripening day by day while picking lasts, `first` of it at once), how many days a ripe crop keeps on the plant and in the kitchen, FAO-56 crop coefficients (ini, mid, end) and p, FAO-33's Ky, a full crop's yield and residue (kg/m²) and RB209's N, P and K uptake (kg/ha), its harvest index (`harvestIndex`: the share of what it takes that's in the eaten part, from RB209's offtake in the produce: salad 0.75, radishes 0.5, lettuce 0.8, beans 0.45, potatoes 0.75, tomatoes 0.6, marigolds none), what a frost does ('plant', 'tops', 'none'), hours to pick a kg, the pests it draws (`docs/systems/pests.md`), and for beans and tomatoes the share of their yield that depends on pollinators (Klein et al. 2007, `pollinated`). French marigolds are the garden's flower (`flower: true`, no food, never in the rotation; `docs/systems/biodiversity.md`).
- **The state.** The crop in a bed is the bed's `crop` lever (`CropState`: which crop, when sown, degree days so far, its evapotranspiration against unstressed, the nutrients it needed and got, kg made, today's Ks, frost damage, and `lost`, the share of its yield lost to pests). The model replaces it each day, never changes it in place, so the snapshot's deltas carry it. Its ripe produce is the bed's `food.<product>` stock (kg), and waste lying on the bed (residue, what bolted or rotted) its `waste` stock (product `greens`). `history` keeps the last four families grown, for the rotation. The crops system refuses a plan on `crop` or `history`.
- **Each day** (the `day` tick): degree days from the day's mean temperature above the base (times `GROWTH_PACE`, 1: the real pace, #11); the day's water stress (FAO-56 eq. 84 on the bed's moisture and the crop's p), added to Σ Ks·Kc·ET₀ and Σ Kc·ET₀ once it's up; nutrients taken from the bed's `nitrate`, `phosphorus` and `potassium` into the plant (the bed's `crop.nitrogen`, `crop.phosphorus` and `crop.potassium`) in step with development, as much as is there (Liebig: the scarcest sets the share got); at maturity a once-crop ripens its whole yield and a repeat crop its first flush, then day by day, each as `ripening` flows from `growth` into the bed's food stock. The yield is the full yield × (1 − Ky(1 − ETa/ETm)) × (1 − 0.8 × the nutrient shortfall) × (1 − frost damage) × (1 − lost to pests), and a pollinated crop's ripening × `pollination()` (`docs/systems/biodiversity.md`); quality (0–100) falls with water and nutrient stress, frost and pests. Ripe produce of a repeat crop left on the plant goes off at its keeping rate; a once-crop left past picking time bolts. Going off is food out to `decay` and waste back in on the bed, with fresh produce's nutrients (from `growth`, where they left as food) into the bed's `waste.*`, counted as wasted in the kitchen's ledger. A crop that's picked, bolted, spent or killed finishes: its residue (`residue` × the bed × how far it grew) comes in from `growth` onto the bed, what it took leaves by its harvest index as food (to `growth`) and the rest stays with the residue (the bed's `waste.nitrogen`, `waste.phosphorus` and `waste.potassium`) for the gardener to carry to the heap (`docs/systems/carbon.md`), and the bed is empty for the next.
- **Frost** (the `hour` tick): when the hour's grass temperature (`hourOf()`, part 2's frost) is below 0 °C, less any cover's degrees (`shelter()`, from `COVERS` in the data, empty until part 6's cold frame adds itself), beans and tomatoes once up are killed (cleared as residue at the day's end), and potatoes' tops are blackened: set back 100 degree days and a tenth of the yield, once a day at most, and not once they're ready (the tubers are made). Salad, radishes and lettuce shrug off a light frost.
- **The plan** (each bed's levers, set by the `plan` command): `sow` is a crop id, `'rotation'` or `'none'`; `sowFrom` is a day of the year the gardener waits for, or null for as soon as it's in season; `dig` (true or false) asks the gardener to dig a plot out of the lawn (part 6's shed shows it; the Garden tab lists dug beds only). `plannedCrop()` is what the plan says to sow on a date: the named crop if in season, or for the rotation the first crop in season of the next family after the last grown (beans, brassicas, potatoes and tomatoes, lettuce, round again). A new game's plan: bed 1 follows the rotation once its overwintered salad is done, and bed 2 is sown with radishes on the first morning (`DEFAULT_PLAN` in `src/sim/state.ts`).
- **The pests' hook** (`harm()`): a pest takes a share more of the crop's yield (up to 95 %), which the pest model sizes by the growth still to come (`remaining()`: seedlings eaten, tops blighted) or by the day's growth (sap). The model notes `growth`, `water stress`, `frost`, `frost damage` and `pollination` as effects for the Explain card (`docs/systems/explain.md`).
- **The water model's hook** (`cropCover()`): the ground the crop covers (10 % at emergence to all of it 70 % of the way to maturity) and its coefficient on FAO-56's curve (flat at ini, up to mid as it covers the ground, down to end while it's picked), used by `groundCoefficient()` for the covered part with the crop's own p.
- **The head start** (the owner's pick on #11): a new game opens with bed 1 holding salad leaves sown the September before (hardy overwintering sorts, as the RHS grows them for late-winter picking), set by `overwintered()` at the new game as an ordinary crop state, 12 degree days short of their first cut (`HEAD_START` in `src/sim/state.ts`). From then on it grows, is picked and finishes like any other.
- **The pace** at the real degree days (`GROWTH_PACE` stays 1), on seeds 1–3: the overwintered salad's first cut picked on day 3–6; radishes sown in bed 2 on day 1 up on about day 7 and ready in about seven weeks; salad sown in March first cut in about a month; lettuce sown on 1 April ready in 8–11 weeks.

## The playable garden (#54)

- **Winter crops** (`winter: true`, `WINTER_IDS`): winter salad leaves, broad beans, garlic, overwintering onions and a green manure of rye and vetch (`dugIn: true`), each with its RHS autumn season. They're a bed's **winter line** (the `winter` plan lever, gated by `garden.winter`): `plannedCrop()` sows the summer plan's crop (`summerCrop()`) and, when that has nothing in season, the winter line's. The `sow` lever takes no winter crop. **Kale** and **leeks** are summer-plan crops picked through the winter.
- **The rotation across the beds:** `summerCrop()` steps on from the bed's last rotation step (onions, garlic and leeks count as the legume step, `STEP_OF`) and, of the steps with something in season, takes the one the other dug beds grow least (the gardener passes them, and what's going in today), so six beds on the rotation grow a mix, not five beds of tomatoes.
- **Covers:** the cold frame (`COVERS['cold-frame']`, `docs/systems/shed.md`) keeps 3 °C of frost off (`shelter()`) and widens its bed's seasons by 21 days each end (`coverDays()`, `inSeason(c, d, extend)`).
- **An idle bed says why:** `nextSowing()` is the next day its plan sows something, which the plan's line shows ("Empty: nothing sows until 1 Mar").
- **Two notes:** `beds full` (every dug bed in use and a plot still under grass) and `empty autumn bed` (a dug bed empty from August to November), which unfold `garden.dig` and `garden.winter`.
- **A green manure dug in** (`finish()` is exported for it): `src/sim/models/carbon.ts`'s `digIn()`.
- The bed's `history` keeps the last eight families.
