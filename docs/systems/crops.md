# Crops

What grows in each dug bed, from sowing to the compost heap (`src/sim/models/crops.ts`, with its plausibility test in `src/sim/models/crops.test.ts`), from the six crops in `src/data/crops.ts`; the water model takes each crop's coefficient (`src/sim/models/water.ts`), the map draws the crops (`src/ui/map/draw.ts`) and the Garden tab shows and sets the plan (`src/ui/GardenTab.tsx`).

- **The crops** (`CROPS`): salad leaves and radishes (brassicas), lettuce (daisy family), French beans (legume), potatoes and tomatoes (solanum). Each has an RHS sowing season (`sow`), sown as seed or planted out (seed potatoes, young tomatoes raised on a windowsill, not modelled), a base temperature, degree days to emergence, to the first harvest and for how long it's picked, 'once' (lettuce, radishes, potatoes: all ready together) or 'repeat' (salad leaves cut and come again, beans, tomatoes: ripening day by day while picking lasts, `first` of it at once), how many days a ripe crop keeps on the plant and in the kitchen, FAO-56 crop coefficients (ini, mid, end) and p, FAO-33's Ky, a full crop's yield and residue (kg/m²) and RB209's N, P and K uptake (kg/ha), what a frost does ('plant', 'tops', 'none'), hours to pick a kg, and the pests it draws (for part 5).
- **The state.** The crop in a bed is the bed's `crop` lever (`CropState`: which crop, when sown, degree days so far, its evapotranspiration against unstressed, the nutrients it needed and got, kg made, today's Ks, frost damage). The model replaces it each day, never changes it in place, so the snapshot's deltas carry it. Its ripe produce is the bed's `food.<product>` stock (kg), and waste lying on the bed (residue, what bolted or rotted) its `waste` stock (product `greens`). `history` keeps the last four families grown, for the rotation. The crops system refuses a plan on `crop` or `history`.
- **Each day** (the `day` tick): degree days from the day's mean temperature above the base (times `GROWTH_PACE`, 1: the real pace, #11); the day's water stress (FAO-56 eq. 84 on the bed's moisture and the crop's p), added to Σ Ks·Kc·ET₀ and Σ Kc·ET₀ once it's up; nutrients taken from the bed's `nitrate`, `phosphorus` and `potassium` to the `growth` boundary in step with development, as much as is there (Liebig: the scarcest sets the share got); at maturity a once-crop ripens its whole yield and a repeat crop its first flush, then day by day, each as `ripening` flows from `growth` into the bed's food stock. The yield is the full yield × (1 − Ky(1 − ETa/ETm)) × (1 − 0.8 × the nutrient shortfall) × (1 − frost damage); quality (0–100) falls with water and nutrient stress and frost. Ripe produce of a repeat crop left on the plant goes off at its keeping rate; a once-crop left past picking time bolts. Going off is food out to `decay` and waste back in on the bed, counted as wasted in the kitchen's ledger. A crop that's picked, bolted, spent or killed finishes: its residue (`residue` × the bed × how far it grew) comes in from `growth` onto the bed and the bed is empty for the next.
- **Frost** (the `hour` tick): when the hour's grass temperature (`hourOf()`, part 2's frost) is below 0 °C, less any cover's degrees (`shelter()`, from `COVERS` in the data, empty until part 6's cold frame adds itself), beans and tomatoes once up are killed (cleared as residue at the day's end), and potatoes' tops are blackened: set back 100 degree days and a tenth of the yield, once a day at most. Salad, radishes and lettuce shrug off a light frost.
- **The plan** (each bed's levers, set by the `plan` command): `sow` is a crop id, `'rotation'` or `'none'`; `sowFrom` is a day of the year the gardener waits for, or null for as soon as it's in season; `dig` (true or false) asks the gardener to dig a plot out of the lawn (part 6's shed shows it; the Garden tab lists dug beds only). `plannedCrop()` is what the plan says to sow on a date: the named crop if in season, or for the rotation the first crop in season of the next family after the last grown (beans, brassicas, potatoes and tomatoes, lettuce, round again). A new game's plan is the founding spec's first card: salad leaves in bed 1, radishes in bed 2.
- **The water model's hook** (`cropCover()`): the ground the crop covers (10 % at emergence to all of it 70 % of the way to maturity) and its coefficient on FAO-56's curve (flat at ini, up to mid as it covers the ground, down to end while it's picked), used by `groundCoefficient()` for the covered part with the crop's own p.
- **The pace** at the real degree days, on seeds 1–3 with the default plan: radish shoots on about day 7 and salad's on day 10–13, the first cut of salad leaves on day 27–34, lettuce sown on 1 April ready in 8–11 weeks. The founding spec's first harvest on day 7 would need `GROWTH_PACE` of about 4: the owner's call (#11).
