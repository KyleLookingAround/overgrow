# Explain

Every effect in the game has a cause and a place, and a card that explains it (the founding spec, "Every effect can be explained"; the owner's win W3): the one entry point in the sim (`src/sim/effects.ts`, tested with the table in `src/sim/effects.test.ts`), the Explain table (`src/data/explain.ts`), the card (`src/ui/Explain.tsx`, in the one overlay `src/ui/Card.tsx`), the page's week of effects (`src/ui/effects-log.ts`), the badges over the map (`src/ui/badges.ts`, `src/ui/Badges.tsx`, placed by `src/ui/map/placement.ts` with its test `src/ui/map/placement.test.ts`), a panel's numbers (`src/ui/Num.tsx`, used in `src/ui/Panel.tsx`, `src/ui/GardenTab.tsx`, `src/ui/KitchenTab.tsx` and `src/ui/TopBar.tsx`), the notices (`src/ui/notices.ts` with `src/ui/notices.test.ts`, and `src/ui/Notices.tsx`), and the map's pulse and creatures (`src/ui/map/renderer.ts`, `src/ui/map/life.ts`, mounted by `src/ui/MapView.tsx` in `src/ui/App.tsx`). The `explain` check holds it on the page.

- **Effects** (`Effect`: kind, cause, amount, unit, and `at`, a node id). Every flow a system moves is one: its `what` is the cause, at the end it reaches if that's drawn on the map, else the end it leaves, else whichever is a node (`placeOf()`); the tick turns its merged flows into effects. An event that moves nothing is noted by its system through `note()`: `frost` (the lawn, °C below zero), `frost damage`, `growth` (degree days), `water stress`, `pollination` (kg the bees added), `drought` and `waterlogging` (the soil model, daily), `slugs`, `aphids` and `blight` (a share of the crop), `Smith period`, `removing blighted leaves`, and `flowers` (m² in bloom). A tick command's events are merged by cause, place and unit, its flows' effects come one each from its merged flows (`flowEffects()`), and both are carried in the snapshot as `effects` (not saved); the kind comes from the table, `'unknown'` for a cause with none.
- **The Explain table** (`src/data/explain.ts`: `CAUSES` holds each entry's kind and causes and `WORDS` what it says, kept apart so the sim, which needs only `KIND_BY_CAUSE`, ships to its worker without the words; `explain()` finds a cause's entry): one entry for one or more causes, each with its kind (weather, water, soil, crop, work, kitchen, carbon, pest, wildlife), a title, what happened, the mechanism, its fast and slow effects, and its source, from the model headers. Entries with no recorded cause explain a number: `soil health`, `money`, `carbon`, `cat`. `src/sim/effects.test.ts` plays two years with every pest policy and fails on any cause with no entry, and on an entry missing a field; a new cause adds its entry in the same PR.
- **The card.** A tap on an effect in a place's panel ("In the last week", the page's log summed by cause over a week of game time, the ten most recent), a badge, a creature on the map (a slug, an aphid cluster, a bee, a ladybird, the cat) or a number with a dotted underline (a place's rows, the gardener's hours, the day's ask, the kitchen's totals, the money, the carbon dial) opens it over the foot of the map: the kind, the title, what happened, the place and its week's amounts, how, now and over time, and the source in the footer. Escape or the close button shuts it; the map pulses a ring round the place while it's open (held still under reduced motion).
- **The badges** (`badgesOf()`): at most two a place, from the snapshot: frost on the lawn; on a dug bed killed by frost, blight, slugs out, aphids over 30 a m², a thirsty crop or dry soil, flowers in bloom; each a 40 px button with its own shape, placed by `placeBadges()` at its place's top-right corner and moved apart, all inside the map, none where its anchor is off the map (the owner's win W15). The layer over the map lets taps through except on a badge, a card or a notice (W12).
- **Notices** (W27): rare, since impacts are on the map; today a command the game refused. At most two, the newest kept, the same text never stacked, and each gone after six seconds unless it's a choice.
