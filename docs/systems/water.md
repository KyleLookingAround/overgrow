# Water

The FAO-56 soil water balance for each bed and the lawn, and the butt filling from the shed's roof (`src/sim/models/water.ts`, with its plausibility test in `src/sim/models/water.test.ts`), on the soils of `src/sim/models/soil.ts`, the crops' coefficients from `src/sim/models/crops.ts` and the roof in `src/data/garden.ts`; the panel shows a place's moisture (`src/ui/Panel.tsx`) and the map colours the dug beds by it (`src/ui/map/draw.ts`).

- **Each step of the balance** (on the `hour` tick; at the garden's hourly steps the balance's own step is three hours, `BALANCE_HOURS`, run at the steps ending on 00:00, 03:00 and every third hour on, so a window never crosses midnight; FAO-56's own balance is daily) the water model takes the step's rain (`rainBetween()`) and its share of the day's reference evapotranspiration (`et0()` times `lightBetween()`: none at night), or at a step of a day or more each day's whole rain and evapotranspiration in turn (from the weather's `step` at a week or a month), and for every place with soil moves, in order: rain in from the `rain` boundary; anything above saturation out to `runoff`; evapotranspiration out to `evapotranspiration`; and a share of what's above field capacity out to `drainage`, half of it in about eight hours and the last half millimetre at once, no faster than the saturated conductivity, with the nitrate it carries (the soil model's `leach()`). Every litre is a flow, so the `conservation` test covers it.
- **Reference evapotranspiration**: FAO Penman-Monteith on the day's maximum, minimum and sunshine, with FAO-56's missing-data rules (dew point at the minimum in a humid climate, wind 2 m/s, radiation from sunshine hours by Ångström); Hargreaves on temperature alone for a day with no sunshine figure. Both are in the file and the test holds them within 40 % of each other on a June day. A hot sunny July day gives 4–6 mm, a January day under 1 mm, and a year at the station about 600–700 mm.
- **The ground's coefficient** (`groundCoefficient()`): grass at FAO-56's cool-season turf (0.9) with its water stress coefficient once half its available water is gone, and bare soil evaporating at up to 1.05 until the surface's readily evaporable water is gone and then less, stopping once its total evaporable water is (FAO-56's REW and TEW, by texture in `src/data/soils.ts`), each in proportion to its share of the place's land. A crop (`cropCover()`) covers part of a bed, from a tenth at emergence to all of it, and that part gives water back at the crop's coefficient for its stage with the crop's own water stress coefficient (its p); the bare rest evaporates as bare soil. So a bare bed stays moist below a dry crust, grass dries deeper, and a bed of potatoes at mid-season (1.15) draws the soil down faster than the reference.
- **The butt.** The shed's roof (3.9 m², about 85 % of its rain reaching the gutter) drains into the butt; above its 200 L cap the rest overflows to `runoff`. The gardener's can takes water out of it (`docs/systems/gardener.md`), then the tap once it's empty.
- **Its speed.** With the soil and the weather, about 0.17 ms a garden game day headless (185 flows a day, most of them the hourly evapotranspiration; about a third of that since the balance's three-hour step, #61), inside the part's 0.25 ms. The step-length test runs the weather, water and soil alone: the gardener's watering belongs to the garden's hourly step.
- **Explain.** Rain, evapotranspiration, drainage, runoff, overflow and watering are effects at each soil and the butt, and a bed's moisture opens its card (`docs/systems/explain.md`).

## The allotment's trough (part 8)

`plotNeed()` and `troughDay()` are the trough's half (`docs/systems/season.md`): a plot's use by FAO-56 over its watered share once its soil has had three dry days, and a day's water shared by first come, equal slots or by need, the mains refilling up to the brim. The weather runs at the allotment for them; the soil water balance doesn't (a plot is a sealed node).

## The playable garden (#54)

A bed under the cold frame (its `cover` lever) gets no rain; the gardener waters it (`docs/systems/shed.md`).
