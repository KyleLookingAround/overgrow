# Unfolding and the first minute

Every system runs from the first hour and the map shows its impacts from day one, but its instruments (numbers, dials, badges, tabs, plan lines, levers) unfold only as the player gains influence over it: never greyed, never all at once, one sign a batch with a short line on why (the owner's principle, `docs/decisions/ADR-2026-09-29-unfolding.md`). The table is `src/data/unfold.ts` (tested in `src/data/unfold.test.ts`); the tick in `src/sim/commands.ts` adds a key to the saved `seen` list the first time one of its causes is recorded (`revealed()`, one lookup an effect), and the snapshot carries `seen` and the page's `settings`. The page reads it in `src/ui/TopBar.tsx`, `src/ui/Panel.tsx`, `src/ui/GardenTab.tsx`, `src/ui/KitchenTab.tsx`, `src/ui/ShedTab.tsx` and `src/ui/badges.ts`; the sign is `unfoldSign()` in `src/ui/notices.ts` (tested in `src/ui/notices.test.ts`), shown by `src/ui/Notices.tsx` from `src/ui/App.tsx`. The first minute's card is `src/ui/FirstPlan.tsx`, the goal bar `src/ui/GoalBar.tsx` with its words in `src/ui/goal.ts` (tested in `src/ui/goal.test.ts`) over the ring `src/sim/goal.ts` keeps, and the shed's first offer `src/data/shed.ts`. The `unfold` and `first-minute` checks hold it on the page.

- **The core**, shown from the first morning: the level, the date and time, the speeds, the gardener's card, each dug bed's sowing line, the places and what they hold (food, waste, compost), the Garden tab and the goal bar.
- **The table, in the order a new player meets it** (seed 1's day in brackets):
  1. `garden.water` (day 1, 07:00): the first watering, drought or water stress. Soil moisture in a bed's panel, the watering line in the plan, the water butt's level, the dry-soil and thirsty-crop badges. Gates `waterBelow`.
  2. `garden.slugs` (day 1, dusk): the first slugs feeding or picked. The slugs' policy line, their numbers and badges. Gates the `slugs` policy.
  3. `garden.shed` (day 1, 21:00): the first time slugs cost the gardener time or money (a torch patrol, traps, pellets). The Shed tab: the gardener's tools and the beer trap, worth having next (buying it is part 6c's).
  4. `garden.kitchen` (day 2, 18:00): the household's first ask of the garden (`ask`, noted from the second evening's meal) or the first meal eaten. The Kitchen tab.
  5. `garden.money` (about day 45): the first sale at the honesty box, or the first thing bought (pellets, a spray). The money in the top bar, the purse in the kitchen's panel and the box's takings.
  6. `garden.aphids` and `garden.flowers` (about day 77): the first aphids arriving (or pollination, or ladybirds, for the flowers). The aphids' policy line and badges; marigolds in the sowing menu, the border checkbox and the flowers' badge. Gate the `aphids` policy, `edge` and sowing marigolds.
  7. `garden.blight` (the first Smith period, day 14 to 91 by seed): the blight policy line, its share and badges. Gates the `blight` policy.
  8. `garden.soil` and `garden.carbon` (about day 105, together): the first compost spread (the first feeding), or the first dig for the carbon. Organic matter, N-P-K and soil health in a bed's panel; the carbon dial, the carbon in the soil and the land. One sign for both.
  9. `garden.weather`: the first frost that reaches a crop it can hurt (`frost damage`). The temperature in the top bar and the frost badge on the lawn (the cold frame's lever with it is part 6c's).
- **Levers are gated in the sim** (`GATES`): a command on one is refused ("that hasn’t come up in the garden yet") until its key has unfolded, so the bot plays the same game; its policies wait for the key (`once()` and `pestPolicy()` in `tools/bot/player.ts`). The first carbon choice, `dig`, is open: it's the choice that unfolds the dial.
- **"Show all details"** (the checkbox at the foot of the Garden tab; `{type: 'setting', key: 'details', value: true | false}`, saved in `settings` and carried to a new game): every number, tab, dial and badge shows at once. Lever lines still wait for their key, because the sim would refuse them: the setting shows numbers, not powers. A key not in the table never shows, whatever the setting.
- **The sign** (win W26): when a snapshot's `seen` grows, the page makes one notice for the whole batch ("New: soil moisture and the watering line", with each key's short line on why), never one each; a long batch (a big tick, a save carried on after a while) names two and counts the rest. It pulses once with a ring, and is a still ring under reduced motion. A new game or a load starts afresh, with no sign.
- **The first minute** (the founding spec, "The first minute"): the page's own new game opens paused on the first plan's card (salad leaves in bed 1, the owner's head start, #11; radishes in bed 2). "Sow them" keeps the plan and "Let them choose" hands bed 2 to the gardener's rotation (the first delegation); either is `{type: 'card', id: 'first-plan', answer: 'accept' | 'choose'}`, which starts the clock at 1× and is answered once a save. The card is up while it's unanswered and the clock hasn't moved. While it's up nothing else shows over the map (win W5's queue): no notice, no goal bar, and a tap on a number or badge waits behind it and opens once it's answered. Then the gardener sows and waters in the first hour, slugs come onto the damp beds at dusk and the gardener goes out with a torch, a tap on a slug opens the first Explain card, the Shed tab shows the beer trap by the next morning, and the kitchen's first ask comes on day 2's evening.
- **The goal bar** (win W18) sits at the foot of the map and gives way to any card or notice: before the first harvest it counts down ("First harvest: salad leaves in Bed 1, 96 % grown"); after, it shows the step-up offer's three numbers as small meters and names the one furthest from its target with what raises it ("Reliability 0 of 60: a steadier mix of crops (14 of 52 weeks so far)"), from `stepUpStatus()` over the ring `src/sim/goal.ts` records each week from the first Monday (the kitchen's `goal` lever, which no command can set: output as kg eaten and sold, the carbon, and the dug beds' soil health). Before the ring's first week it says the committee looks at the whole year. The "met" latch is the offer's (part 7).
- **One "try faster" nudge** (win W17): after the first harvest, at 1×, past the first minute (60 game hours), once a save: "Try 2×". Any way of reaching 2× or 4× answers it (the `speed` command marks `card.try-faster` seen), as does its button or closing it (`{type: 'card', id: 'try-faster', answer: 'yes' | 'no'}`). The bot never touches the speed, and the headless run has no page, so it never shows there.
- **Each new level starts simple again:** its keys are its own (`allotment.…`), unfolding from zero; a sealed level shows only its tile's numbers.
