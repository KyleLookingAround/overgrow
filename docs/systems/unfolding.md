# Unfolding and the first minute

Every system runs from the first hour and the map shows its impacts from day one, but its instruments (numbers, dials, badges, tabs, plan lines, levers) unfold only as the player gains influence over it: never greyed, never all at once, one sign a batch with a short line on why (the owner's principle, `docs/decisions/ADR-2026-09-29-unfolding.md`). The table is `src/data/unfold.ts` (tested in `src/data/unfold.test.ts`); the tick in `src/sim/commands.ts` adds a key to the saved `seen` list the first time one of its causes is recorded (`revealed()`, one lookup an effect), and the snapshot carries `seen` and the page's `settings`. The keys are named by level and system (`garden.water`), or by the node beside the level for the household's (`household.commute`). The page reads it in `src/ui/TopBar.tsx`, `src/ui/Panel.tsx`, `src/ui/GardenTab.tsx`, `src/ui/KitchenTab.tsx`, `src/ui/ShedTab.tsx` and `src/ui/badges.ts`; the sign is `unfoldSign()` in `src/ui/notices.ts` (tested in `src/ui/notices.test.ts`), shown by `src/ui/Notices.tsx` from `src/ui/App.tsx`. The first minute's card is `src/ui/FirstPlan.tsx`, the goal bar `src/ui/GoalBar.tsx` with its words in `src/ui/goal.ts` (tested in `src/ui/goal.test.ts`) over the ring `src/sim/goal.ts` keeps, and the shed's first offer `src/data/shed.ts`. The `unfold` and `first-minute` checks hold it on the page.

- **The core**, shown from the first morning: the level, the date and time, the speeds, the gardener's card, each dug bed's sowing line, the places and what they hold (food, waste, compost), the Garden tab and the goal bar.
- **The table, in the order a new player meets it** (seed 1's day in brackets):
  1. `garden.water` (day 1, 07:00): the first watering, drought or water stress. Soil moisture in a bed's panel, the watering line in the plan, the water butt's level, the dry-soil and thirsty-crop badges. Gates `waterBelow`.
  2. `garden.slugs` (day 1, dusk): the first slugs feeding or picked. The slugs' policy line, their numbers and badges. Gates the `slugs` policy.
  3. `garden.shed` (day 1, 21:00): the first time slugs cost the gardener time or money (a torch patrol, traps, pellets). The Shed tab: the gardener's tools, and the beer traps to buy (`shed.beer-trap`, the same trigger).
  4. `garden.kitchen` (day 2, 18:00): the household's first ask of the garden (`ask`, noted from the second evening's meal) or the first meal eaten. The Kitchen tab.
  5. `household.commute` (day 2, 18:00, with the kitchen): the gardener home from work (`commute`, noted from the second day's homecoming at 17:30, which falls in the same hour's tick as the kitchen's first ask, so the two share one sign and the first minute gets none of its own though they leave for work on day 1). The job's line on the gardener's card.
  6. `garden.money` (day 5, 17:30): the first payday (`wages`, the gardener home with the weekly shop on Friday), or the first sale at the honesty box, or the first thing bought (pellets, a spray), whichever comes first. The money in the top bar, the purse in the kitchen's panel, the box's takings and the last week's pay, shop and rest of life in the Kitchen tab.
  7. `household.groceries` (day 12): the first weekly shop the garden fed the household some of (`groceries saved`). Groceries saved in the Kitchen tab, the last week's and since the start.
  8. `garden.aphids` and `garden.flowers` (about day 77): the first aphids arriving (or pollination, or ladybirds, for the flowers). The aphids' policy line and badges; marigolds in the sowing menu, the border checkbox and the flowers' badge. Gate the `aphids` policy, `edge` and sowing marigolds.
  9. `garden.blight` (the first Smith period, day 14 to 91 by seed): the blight policy line, its share and badges. Gates the `blight` policy.
  10. `garden.soil`, `garden.carbon` and `household.footprint` (day 129, together; day 107 before part 6b, when the kitchen's bigger ask sent more of the garden's produce to go off and onto the heap): the first compost spread (the first feeding), or the first dig for the carbon. Organic matter, N-P-K and soil health in a bed's panel; the carbon dial, the carbon in the soil and the land; and under the dial's figure, a bag with the shop food's footprint since the start, which is never added to the dial. One sign for all three.
  11. `garden.weather`: the first frost that reaches a crop it can hurt (`frost damage`). The temperature in the top bar and the frost badge on the lawn (and the cold frame in the shed, `shed.cold-frame`).
- **Levers are gated in the sim** (`GATES`): a command on one is refused ("that hasn’t come up in the garden yet") until its key has unfolded, so the bot plays the same game; its policies wait for the key (`once()` and `pestPolicy()` in `tools/bot/player.ts`). The first carbon choice, `dig`, is open: it's the choice that unfolds the dial.
- **"Show all details"** (the checkbox at the foot of the Garden tab; `{type: 'setting', key: 'details', value: true | false}`, saved in `settings` and carried to a new game): every number, tab, dial and badge shows at once. Lever lines still wait for their key, because the sim would refuse them: the setting shows numbers, not powers. A key not in the table never shows, whatever the setting.
- **The sign** (win W26): when a snapshot's `seen` grows, the page makes one notice for the whole batch ("New: soil moisture and the watering line", with each key's short line on why), never one each; a long batch (a big tick, a save carried on after a while) names two and counts the rest. It pulses once with a ring, and is a still ring under reduced motion. A new game or a load starts afresh, with no sign.
- **The first minute** (the founding spec, "The first minute"): the page's own new game opens paused on the first plan's card (salad leaves in bed 1, the owner's head start, #11; radishes in bed 2). "Sow them" keeps the plan and "Let them choose" hands bed 2 to the gardener's rotation (the first delegation); either is `{type: 'card', id: 'first-plan', answer: 'accept' | 'choose'}`, which starts the clock at 1× and is answered once a save. The card is up while it's unanswered and the clock hasn't moved. While it's up nothing else shows over the map (win W5's queue): no notice, no goal bar, and a tap on a number or badge waits behind it and opens once it's answered. Then the gardener sows and waters in the first hour, slugs come onto the damp beds at dusk and the gardener goes out with a torch, a tap on a slug opens the first Explain card, the Shed tab shows the beer trap by the next morning, and the kitchen's first ask comes on day 2's evening.
- **The goal bar** (win W18; round two made it one verb and a ring, below) sits at the foot of the map and gives way to any card or notice: before the first harvest it counts down ("First harvest: salad leaves in Bed 1, 96 % grown"); after, it shows the step-up offer's three numbers as small meters and names the one furthest from its target with what raises it ("Reliability 0 of 60: a steadier mix of crops (14 of 52 weeks so far)"), from `stepUpStatus()` over the ring `src/sim/goal.ts` records each week from the game's first day (round two) (the kitchen's `goal` lever, which no command can set: output as kg eaten and sold, the carbon, and the dug beds' soil health). Before the ring's first week it says the committee looks at the whole year. The "met" latch is the offer's (part 7).
- **One "try faster" nudge** (win W17): at 1×, once the first minute (60 game hours) is over (round two: no longer after the first harvest), once a save: "Try 2×". Any way of reaching 2× or 4× answers it (the `speed` command marks `card.try-faster` seen), as does its button or closing it (`{type: 'card', id: 'try-faster', answer: 'yes' | 'no'}`). The bot never touches the speed, and the headless run has no page, so it never shows there.
- **Each new level starts simple again:** its keys are its own (`allotment.…`), unfolding from zero; a sealed level shows only its tile's numbers.

## The playable garden (#54)

Eight more keys: `shed.beer-trap` (with the Shed tab), `garden.dig` (`beds full`, or the first dig), `shed.hose` (`long watering`), `shed.water-butt` (`butt dry`), `shed.compost-bin` (the first compost spread), `shed.nematodes` (`slugs thriving`), `garden.winter` (`empty autumn bed`) and `shed.cold-frame` (`frost damage` or `empty autumn bed`). A `buy` is refused until its `shed.<id>` key has unfolded; the `winter` lever is gated by `garden.winter`; `garden.money` also unfolds with a purchase. The `digging` command itself stays open from the start. `CARDS.year` is the level's-end card, answered once a save.

## The playable garden, round two

- **The goal bar** is one line: a progress ring (the first crop's growth before the first harvest, then the year's share so far times how near the three requirements are), the one thing to do as a verb (`goalLine()`'s `verb`: the next action from `nextStep()`, or what raises the requirement holding it back), and the prize as an icon with "A plot at the allotment" (the words hide on a narrow map; the icon's title says the whole prize). A tap opens the three requirements in plain words (`PLAIN`: how well the garden fed the household, how steadily, the soil), each with a meter and its value against the target. `nextStep()` is the next action with its commands, offered only when the player can act on it (a buy or a dig the purse can pay for, a lever that has come up); the `tips` bot does exactly that and nothing else.
- **The goal's year** counts from the game's first day (`startGoal()`), so the first Monday's sample takes in the days before it and a garden that does well can meet the offer inside its first year.
- **The try-faster nudge** comes once the first minute (60 game hours) is over, before the wait for the spring sowings, without waiting on a harvest; the head start's first cut comes on day 2 or 3 (`HEAD_START.ddToGo` 5).
- **Notices** each carry their game day: an informational one not shown by its day's end is dropped, never shown on a later day (`today()`), while a choice waits to be answered; an opened notice's line sits under a rule, and a screen reader hears a stop between the title and its line.
- **The choices** in the queue after the notices: the try-faster nudge, the most pressing of the week's decisions (`src/ui/decisions.ts`, `docs/systems/shed.md`), and the bed card (`src/ui/bed-card.ts`, `docs/systems/crops.md`), one shown at a time.
