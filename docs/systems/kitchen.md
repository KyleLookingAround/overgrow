# The kitchen

The household's daily ask of the garden, what met it, the shop's food in the cupboard, and the honesty box at the gate (`src/sim/models/kitchen.ts`, with its plausibility test in `src/sim/models/kitchen.test.ts`), from the keeping rule and the box in `src/data/kitchen.ts` and the basket in `src/data/household.ts`; the gate is a place in `src/data/garden.ts`; the Kitchen tab shows it (`src/ui/KitchenTab.tsx`).

- **The ask** (`kitchenAsk(people)`, `askOf(graph)`): the weekly basket's veg over seven days for the household's people (the household node's `members`, `docs/systems/household.md`), by DEFRA Family Food's groups across what the garden grows: potatoes, salads (salad leaves, lettuce, radishes), tomatoes and green veg (beans). It's about 1 kg a day for an average household of 2.4 (NHS's five 80 g portions each, ONS); the garden's household is the gardener alone until the partner (part 10), so 0.42 kg: potatoes 157 g, salads 121 g, tomatoes and green veg 64 g each.
- **The meal** (the `hour` tick, at 18:00; every day of a longer step): each group eats its ask from the kitchen's `food.<product>` stocks, the shortest-keeping first, to the `eaten` boundary; then a group with more stands in for one that's short, up to twice its own ask. What's met is the day's share, the garden's alone. Then what the garden didn't meet comes from the shop's veg in the cupboard (`food.shop-veg`), and the rest of the diet (fruit, bread and cereals, dairy and eggs, meat and fish, everything else) from the shop's (`food.shop-food`), a seventh of the week's basket a day, both as `shop food eaten`. The shop's food doesn't go off before it's eaten. From the second evening the meal notes the day's `ask` (the effect that unfolds the Kitchen tab, `docs/systems/unfolding.md`); the first evening's meal is counted like any other.
- **Going off.** After the meal, what's in the kitchen goes off at 1/(its kitchen keeping days) a day, out as food to `decay` and back as the kitchen's `waste` (the gardener carries it to the heap); what's in the honesty box goes off the same way and is thrown out. Both count as wasted.
- **The honesty box** (the `gate` node, "Honesty box"): the gardener carries what the kitchen won't eat while it's fresh (a group's stretched ask for half each product's shelf life, up to three weeks: `surplus()`) there; each evening passers-by take up to 2 kg (3.5 at weekends) at £2.50 a kg, the best first, food to the `sold` boundary and money from `sold` into the kitchen's `money`, the household's purse. The best is by quality: the kitchen and the box each keep a `quality` lever, 0–100 by product, mixed by weight from each pick's crop quality (`recordPick`, `mixQuality`) and carried to the box with the produce, so stressed produce is left to go off (#29's Q8 (a), the seed of cosmetic standards). No command can set it.
- **The ledger** (the kitchen's `ledger` lever, carried in the snapshot as `kitchen`): the last meal's day, ask, what it ate by product and the share met, the last seven days' shares, and since the start: picked, eaten, wasted (going off anywhere, and produce rotting unpicked on the beds), sold and earned, with the game hours of the first harvest and the first sale (the bot's milestones). The kitchen system refuses a plan on it.
- **What two beds do:** salad leaves and radishes meet about a tenth of the ask in spring; potatoes lifted in summer with salad beside them meet about half of it or more for a week or two (most of it on seeds 2 and 3); the founding spec's "half the kitchen's need" in the first weeks needs more beds.
- **Explain.** The Kitchen tab's ask and totals open their Explain cards, and eating, going off and the honesty box are effects at the kitchen and the gate (`docs/systems/explain.md`).

## The playable garden (#54)

What keeps for two months or more (potatoes, garlic, onions) is stored for up to five months of the household's ask (`STORE` in `src/data/kitchen.ts`), not three weeks, so it's eaten through the winter rather than sold at the gate.

## The playable garden, round two

- **Eggs and soft fruit** (`EXTRAS` in `src/data/kitchen.ts`): the hens' eggs and the fruit cage's berries are eaten at the meal in place of some of the rest of the diet (up to 0.25 kg of eggs a person a week against the dairy and eggs group, and up to the whole kilo of fruit), so the shop's `food.shop-food` eaten falls by the same kg and the household's tally counts them against their groups, which the next shop buys less of (`docs/systems/household.md`). They count as eaten in the ledger (so in the garden's Output), not in the veg ask's share met. Past what the household eats while they keep (eggs a month, berries four days), the gardener takes them to the honesty box, which sells eggs at £5 a kg and berries at £8, the rest at £2.50.

- **A glut** (`GLUT`, `GlutPolicy`): when the day's plan finds more than 3 kg over the kitchen's need, the gardener notes `glut` and the ledger keeps when this glut began (`glutFrom`, a new one after five days without) and was last seen (`glutAt`). What happens to it is the kitchen's `glut` policy lever (`policy` command, or the glut card): `sell` at the box (the default, as before); `preserve` (the gardener freezes, bottles or pickles it in the kitchen, 25 minutes and 30p a kg, into `food.preserves`, up to a freezer drawer's 20 kg; preserves keep about ten months and are eaten at the meal after the fresh veg and its stretch, counted as veg met and as green veg in the household's tally); or `give` (carried over the fence by the gate to a neighbour, to the `given` boundary, counted as `given` and as the neighbour's `goodwill`, the allotment's currency later). A household that preserves or gives keeps only three days of what doesn't store (`PRESERVE.freshDays`), so less goes off in the kitchen (WRAP). What's over the freezer's room still goes to the box.
