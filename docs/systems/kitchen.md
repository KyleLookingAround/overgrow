# The kitchen

The household's daily ask of the garden, what met it, and the honesty box at the gate (`src/sim/models/kitchen.ts`, with its plausibility test in `src/sim/models/kitchen.test.ts`), from the ask, the keeping rule and the box in `src/data/kitchen.ts`; the gate is a place in `src/data/garden.ts`; the Kitchen tab shows it (`src/ui/KitchenTab.tsx`).

- **The ask:** about 1 kg of veg a day for a household of 2.4 eating five 80 g portions each (NHS, ONS), split by DEFRA Family Food's rough shares across what the garden grows: potatoes 0.4 kg, salads (salad leaves, lettuce, radishes) 0.3, tomatoes 0.15, green veg (beans) 0.15.
- **The meal** (the `hour` tick, at 18:00; every day of a longer step): each group eats its ask from the kitchen's `food.<product>` stocks, the shortest-keeping first, to the `eaten` boundary; then a group with more stands in for one that's short, up to twice its own ask. What's met is the day's share. From the second evening the meal notes the day's `ask` (the effect that unfolds the Kitchen tab, `docs/systems/unfolding.md`); the first evening's meal is counted like any other.
- **Going off.** After the meal, what's in the kitchen goes off at 1/(its kitchen keeping days) a day, out as food to `decay` and back as the kitchen's `waste` (the gardener carries it to the heap); what's in the honesty box goes off the same way and is thrown out. Both count as wasted.
- **The honesty box** (the `gate` node, "Honesty box"): the gardener carries what the kitchen won't eat while it's fresh (a group's stretched ask for half each product's shelf life, up to three weeks: `surplus()`) there; each evening passers-by take up to 2 kg (3.5 at weekends) at £2.50 a kg, food to the `sold` boundary and money from `sold` into the kitchen's `money`, the household's purse.
- **The ledger** (the kitchen's `ledger` lever, carried in the snapshot as `kitchen`): the last meal's day, ask, what it ate by product and the share met, the last seven days' shares, and since the start: picked, eaten, wasted (going off anywhere, and produce rotting unpicked on the beds), sold and earned, with the game hours of the first harvest and the first sale (the bot's milestones). The kitchen system refuses a plan on it.
- **What two beds do:** salad leaves and radishes meet about a tenth of the ask in spring; potatoes lifted in summer with salad beside them meet about half of it or more for a week or two (most of it on seeds 2 and 3); the founding spec's "half the kitchen's need" in the first weeks needs more beds.
- **Explain.** The Kitchen tab's ask and totals open their Explain cards, and eating, going off and the honesty box are effects at the kitchen and the gate (`docs/systems/explain.md`).
