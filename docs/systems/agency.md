# Agency and trust

Other people as agents: a household, skills, weighted goals, a habit and a hidden integrity; what they do each week; a helper's take against what they report; watching, and what it costs; goodwill and trust as slow stocks; and an adviser with an interest. Written ahead of parts 8 to 10 (`src/sim/models/agency.ts`, with its plausibility test in `src/sim/models/agency.test.ts` and its numbers in `src/data/agency.ts`). The committee's votes are the other half (`docs/systems/committee.md`). Part 8 wired it: the `agency` system runs at the allotment (level 2), and `docs/systems/season.md` says how the level uses it.

- **Agents** (`Agent`, `makeAgent`, `allotment`): a person has a household from `household.ts` (`householdOf` builds one from a shape: the plot-holder, a partner, a child), skills (labour's `sowing`, `harvest`, `general`), goals (weights on harvest, rest, standing and money that sum to 1, each strayed from its habit's by up to 30 %), one of the spec's four habits (tidy, lazy, generous, competitive) and a hidden `integrity`, 0 to 1. `allotment(rng)` draws the eleven neighbours (three tidy, three lazy, two generous, three competitive, names invented) and shuffles which holds which of plots 2 to 12; the player's is plot 1. Honesty is drawn around 0.72 with the habit's shift (competitive lower, tidy higher).
- **Their week** (`plotHours`, `weekPlan`): a neighbour gives their plot 12 % of their household's garden hours (`weekGardenHours`), times the habit's effort (lazy 0.85, tidy 1.1), spread ±20 % by the week's luck from the `Rng`. `keptness` (household.ts) turns the hours into how kept the plot is. A lone full-time worker gives about 3.8 hours, 64 % of what a ten-rod plot needs; a retired couple give more than it needs.
- **The neglected plot** (`allotment(...).neglected`, `neglectedPlot(seed)`): the plot whose household spends the fewest hours on it. Exactly one of the eleven households lives alone in full-time work, so exactly one has the least time, and neglect follows from time, not a script (Q1, Q13). Part 9 calls `neglectedPlot(seed)` for the plot and its holder; the zoom back in keeps its scripted timing, this only says where the slugs come from. The seed shuffles which plot it is; who it is stays the same neighbour.
- **The helper** (`allotment(...).helper`): the generous or competitive neighbour with the most spare hours (`spareHours`: a tenth of their garden hours less their wish to rest), never the neglected one, with honesty capped at 0.6 so the first lesson lands.
- **A report isn't the truth** (`helperWeek`, `honesty`, `reportOf`): a helper on the second plot takes an agreed third of the harvest, and beyond it what their appetite (the habit's `grab`, 1.6 for the competitive) and their dishonesty let them, spread by the week's chance. Their report is the agreed share plus the hidden part times `honesty = 1 − (1 − integrity)(1 − watch)`: an honest person tells all, an unwatched liar tells none of it. What they took moves from the plot's shelves to theirs as flows (`taken home`, by product), so the map can show the barrow while the numbers show the report; `takings` keeps the truth (`hidden`) for tests and the bot and is not for a panel. The same gap goes the other way for a hired hand: `padded(worked, slack, integrity, watch)` is the hours they claim.
- **Watching** (`WATCH`, `audited`): three settings. Trusting costs nothing and closes none of the gap. A glance costs half an hour a week and 1 % of their goodwill, closes 35 % of the gap; an audit costs two hours and 4 %, closes 80 %, and deters some of the taking (60 % of what it closes). The player's hours go through a flow from their `hours` stock to the `time` boundary. An audit also teaches: trust, the player's belief in their reports, moves towards how candid the person would have been unwatched (`candour`), so an honest helper comes to be believed and a liar is found, and being found costs more goodwill, the more the bigger the gap.
- **A trade with no best option** (`secondPlot`): alone, the second plot costs six hours a week and all its harvest is yours; with a helper it costs your watching hours and a third of the harvest plus what they take unseen. Whether that pays depends on what an hour is worth to you (`hourKg`): an honest helper wins when hours are dear and loses when they're cheap; a dishonest one is worse than an honest one at every price; auditing pays when hours are cheap and someone is taking, and not when they're dear or the helper is honest. The tests show each both ways.
- **Goodwill and trust** (`Relation`, `after`, `drift`, `audited`, `siteGoodwill`): both 0 to 1 and slow. Goodwill (theirs towards you) moves with kept and missed promises (a missed one costs twice a kept one earns), kg shared at the swap shed (capped a week), help given by the hour, a rescue of your own plot's outbreak (up to 0.06, less the later it came), carbon choices (Q14: a bonfire −0.05, peat −0.03, a plot dug from grass −0.04; compost and no-dig +0.02) and being watched. Each step shrinks nearer 0 or 1, so there's no cliff, and goodwill drifts back to neutral by 2.6 % a week (about half in half a year). Trust starts at 0.5 and only audits move it. The site's goodwill is the mean across neighbours: the committee's.
- **An adviser with an interest** (`score`, `recommend`, `tilt`): the seed catalogue scores its own seeds 30 % above their merit; `recommend` is the top `n` by that score and `tilt` compares it with an honest list and names the own seeds it pushed in, which is what the Explain card shows. Across random lists of twenty seeds its top five carry about four of its own seeds against an honest five's two or three.
- **The same people one level up** (`asWorker`): a person is a labour.ts `Worker` for part 13's first hire (a strong wish to rest becomes an hours cap of six a day); their honesty is theirs, and `padded` is what they claim. At the parish and the boards the same `Agent` has a bigger household or none, goals over the same wants and a habit for how they lobby; a hired hand's hidden action (hours, care, what leaves with the harvest) is the same gap. At 5 people you don't choose; at 7 you serve whoever is elected, and the committee's members are agents.
- **The system** (`agency`): a `week` tick plans each agent's week (their `week` lever), drifts their goodwill, and for a node with a `helping` lever works the plot it names: the helper's take moves off the plot's `food.*` stocks onto theirs, a watch takes the player's hours, and `relation` and `takings` update. It refuses a plan on `agent`, `relation` or `takings`: a person's honesty is not a lever.
- **Speed** (Node 22, after a warm-up, from the test's timer): a week for eleven neighbours and a helper about 30–60 µs; `allotment(rng)` about a tenth of that. Far inside the reserve.

## Wiring

**Part 8 (the allotment's first season), wired** (`docs/systems/season.md`). What was planned, and where it went:
- A node per neighbour (kind `neighbour`, on the map at their plot) with the levers `agent` (an `Agent`, from `allotment(rng)`), `relation` (`newRelation()`), `takings` and `week`, and food stocks `food.<product>` where the helper's take lands. Edges carrying `kgFood` from the second plot to the helper's node. `agency` is listed in `src/sim/systems.ts` at level 2. A helper is one node with a `helping` lever (`{plot, watching, payer}`); the `helper` and `watch` commands (`src/sim/season.ts`) set it. `foods()` also reads a sealed plot's `food` stock, so the helper takes from the second plot's shelf.
- Each plot's `kept` (their `week` lever) drives how it looks and what it yields; untended plots get weeds and send pests next door through `pests.ts`'s `spreadPressure`. The slugs in the player's own garden at the scripted time from `neglectedPlot(seed)` are still part 9's.
- The helper's barrow is an activity carrying what they took, measured against what they reported (the `taken home` flow's week). Show `reported` and never `hidden`. Show goodwill as a face or a colour, not a number, until the panel unfolds.
- Add to `graph.ts`: nothing needed. Opinion (goodwill, trust) is kept in levers, not stocks, so it isn't conserved; the `support` unit is the committee's.
- Saved state adds the neighbour nodes' levers (version 12; an older save starts a new game).
- `unfold.ts` keys: `agency.helper` (the second plot offered, with the helper's offer, watching and their report), `agency.trust` (the first week of an audit) and `agency.goodwill` (the first swap at the swap shed; the first carbon choice a neighbour sees joins it in part 10).

**Part 9 (the zoom back in), wired** (`docs/systems/zoom.md`): `neglectedPlot(seed)` for the slugs' plot and its holder; a rescue moves every neighbour's goodwill by the `rescued` event (0.06 × the rescue's share). Still to wire: the seed catalogue's `recommend` and `tilt` on the seed tab's adviser, with the Explain card naming the bias and the own seeds it pushed in.

**Part 10 (the allotment's years):** the swap shed shares surplus (`after(r, {type: 'shared', kg})` across neighbours, weighted by each habit's `give`); carbon choices call `after` on every relationship and so move the committee's goodwill (`docs/systems/committee.md`).

**Part 13 (the smallholding's hire):** `asWorker(agent)` for the first hired hand; `padded` and `honesty` for their claimed hours; `payWages` and `wageCost` in labour.ts stay the wage. Watching is the same lever at the same three settings.

## How it fits and grows

1. **Born where.** Hands-on at the allotment (level 2): each neighbour a household node with a plot; flows are the kg they take and share, the hours a watch costs and the plot hours they give.
2. **Across the ladder.** A sealed allotment carries its goodwill (a Health part). One level up the hired hand and the parish reuse the agents; at 5 people you don't choose; at 7 you serve whoever is elected.
3. **Loops.** The agency loop (delegating buys time, every delegate has interests, watching costs time again) and, through Q14, the carbon loop (a bonfire costs goodwill).
4. **People.** Eleven neighbours with names, habits and goals; the helper; the adviser with an interest.
5. **The lever.** Take on the second plot, accept or refuse the helper, watch or audit them, share at the swap shed.
6. **The map.** The helper carrying a little more than their share (shown, not told); neglected plots going to weeds.
7. **Explain.** The principal–agent problem; why a report isn't the truth; the catalogue's bias as a number.
8. **Economy and balance.** The kg a helper takes (about a third of the harvest agreed, up to a further third or so unseen) and the hours an audit costs; wiring will shift the bot's second-plot and first-swap milestones and its hours at the allotment: measure with the bot on seeds 1 to 3 then.
9. **Carbon and land.** A plot dug from grass, peat and bonfires cost goodwill; compost and no-dig earn it.
10. **Polish.** At 320 px the neighbours' list is a column of names with a face each, the helper's report a line; concise UK English.
11. **The lesson.** A report isn't the truth, and watching costs time.
12. **Unfolding.** Trust with the first helper, as the proposed keys above.
