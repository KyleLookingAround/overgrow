# The clock

One clock for every level, in game hours since the start (`src/sim/clock.ts`), with the ladder's rates in `src/data/ladder.ts`.

- **Steps.** The sim advances in fixed steps of the level's `stepHours`: an hour at levels 1 and 2, a day at 3 to 5, a week at 6 and 7, a month at 8. A `tick` command runs whole steps (any part of a step left over is dropped).
- **The calendar.** Day 1 is 06:00 on Monday 15 March, early spring, on a UTC calendar so every date repeats. `calendar(hours)` gives the date, weekday, time, day of the year, season (meteorological: spring from 1 March) and the game year, counted from 1 and turning on the anniversary of the start. The top bar shows it.
- **Ticks.** Each step fires `hour`, and `day`, `week`, `season` or `year` when it crosses midnight, Monday midnight, the first of March, June, September or December, or the anniversary (`ticksCrossed()`). A system handles the ticks it needs; an hourly system at level 3 is called once a day with `dt` 24.
- **Systems.** A system is its own file exporting a `System` (`name`, `on` by tick, and optionally `command` for the commands it owns), listed in `src/sim/systems.ts` in the order they run. Adding one adds a line there and edits no other system. Each gets its own dice every tick (`systemRng()`, from the seed, its name, the tick and the hour), so adding a system never changes another's draws. Part 1 lists none.
- **Speeds.** Pause, 1×, 2× and 4× (`SPEEDS`), set by the `speed` command and saved. `hoursPerSecond(level, speed)` is 24 ÷ the level's seconds per day × the speed: the garden's 24 s a day is an hour a second at 1×.
- **The real-time loop** (`src/app/clock-loop.ts`) turns real seconds into steps on the page. It keeps the sim up to two steps ahead of what's shown and moves a view time between the two snapshots either side of it, which the map interpolates at. Pausing freezes the view at once; under `prefers-reduced-motion` the view jumps from snapshot to snapshot. A gap between frames longer than 0.25 s counts as 0.25 s, and a hidden tab gets no frames, so the game doesn't run while the page is closed or hidden. If a command moves the sim more than four steps ahead (the checks do), the view jumps to it.
