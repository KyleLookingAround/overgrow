- **Lessons:**
  - **Spring is the ladder's ceiling.** A player's spare money in March and April is £3–4 a week after seed, so no price list gives a rung every two to four weeks then without raising the household's income, which decision 19 ruled out. Measure a rung spacing target against income by month, not by year. → The PR states it as the honest figure.
  - **Unfold tweaks move the dice.** Unfolding the bee hotel or the cloches earlier bought them sooner and lost seed 1's offer by a week. A change to when things unfold is a balance change: run all three seeds before keeping it.
  - **The goal bar's requirement steps bypassed the ladder** (the greenhouse at £292 to go while a £20 rung was on offer): a big buy's step now gives way to a cheaper rung. A tip that names a purchase should read the same ordered list as the shop.
