# Brief: part 3 of the first slice, crops and the gardener

The founding spec (`docs/specs/overgrow.md`) splits the first slice into fifteen parts. Part 1 (#5) laid the graph, the clock, saves and the page's shell; part 2 (#7) the weather, soil and water under the garden. This is part 3: things grow, and the gardener does the work. Part 4 (the bot) runs side by side with this part from the same `main`; this part owns the game code, part 4 owns the bot, and **this part merges first**.

## Goal and what it may touch

- **Deliver** the founding spec's part 3 ("The first roadmap", item 3): growth, sowing, watering and harvesting done by the gardener under the plan, with their hours as the constraint and each job drawn; the kitchen's demand and the honesty box, with produce carried; carbon and land counted; and the `garden` check. Branch `feature/crops-and-gardener` from `main`, one PR.
- **The spec is its spec.** The founding spec's garden paragraph ("The garden": the gardener, "Crops", "Harvest and demand", "Carbon and land", "The plan"), the systems map rows for "Crops", "Markets, prices and demand" (the kitchen), "Waste and circularity" and "Greenhouse gases", "Things move" for the garden, "Activities", and "The first roadmap" item 3 are approved; this brief approves them in advance for this part (the `feature` playbook, step 2), so no new spec file. Start from a GitHub issue (the Feature template) and link it from the PR.
- **What it builds, concretely:**
  - **Crops** (`crops.ts` in `src/sim/models/`, data in `crops.ts` in `src/data/`): salad leaves, radishes, lettuce, beans, potatoes and tomatoes, each with a sowing window (RHS sowing calendars), degree days to maturity from a base temperature (growing-degree-day phenology), FAO-56 crop coefficients by stage (filling the ground-coefficient hook part 2 left in the water model, for the ground a crop covers), a yield response to water stress (FAO-56's Ky), nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium taken from the soil stocks part 2 laid down (RB209 for the sizes), a family for rotation, the pests it draws (a field for part 5), a harvest window after which it bolts or rots, and quality from stress. Frost kills tender crops (beans, potatoes' tops, tomatoes) on part 2's frost; leave a plain hook for part 6's cold frame. Yield and quality are real kilograms by product, moved as flows. Crop residues and what bolts or rots go to the compost heap as flows.
  - **The gardener** (their own file in `src/sim/`, data for job times in `src/data/`): one person with about four hours a day for the garden, more at weekends (the owner confirmed four, #2). Each day they work through the jobs the plan and the garden call for, in a sensible order (harvest what's ready, water what's below the plan's line, sow what's due, carry produce), each job taking time with the tools they have (a watering can from the butt, then the tap when the butt is empty: a trip per can, the time and the litres real); what doesn't fit waits until tomorrow. Every job is an **activity** (part 1's shape): the gardener walks to the place, does it, and walks on, and each trip is a new activity id (`docs/lessons/5-graph-and-clock.md`: a reused id vanishes under deltas). Watering moves litres from the butt or the `mains` boundary to the bed as flows. Leave the job-time table keyed by tool so part 6's hose, drip lines and wheelbarrow change times without editing this file.
  - **The plan** (a `plan` command on part 1's lever shape, and the Garden tab): what to sow in each dug bed and when, or "follow the rotation" (a simple family rotation the gardener follows); and the soil moisture below which the gardener waters. The pest policy is part 5's; leave room for it in the tab. "Let them choose" is part 6's adviser.
  - **The kitchen** (`kitchen.ts` in `src/sim/models/`): the household wants about 1 kg of veg a day in a mix (DEFRA Family Food for the size and mix), eats from what's picked, and what's surplus goes to an honesty box at the gate, sold at a plain per-kg price into the household's money (a `sold` flow). Demand met, food wasted and money earned are in the snapshot. The Kitchen tab shows the day's ask and what met it.
  - **Carbon and land** (`carbon.ts` in `src/sim/models/`): the compost heap stores carbon and emits some as it breaks down (IPCC composting factors), and compost the gardener spreads adds to a bed's organic matter through part 2's soil stocks; digging a bed from lawn is a land-use change (part 1's flow from `land.grass` to `land.crops`) with its soil carbon effect (IPCC land-use guidelines, rough). The top bar's dial reads it all from the air node. A bag of peat compost's carbon is part 6's (the shed); leave the flow's shape.
  - **On the map** (the living map, the spec's "Things move", garden): the gardener walking to each job with the can, sowing, watering (the soil darkening under part 2's drawing), picking, and carrying produce to the kitchen and the gate; crops drawn by stage, wilting under water stress, and blackened by frost. Everything drawn from activities and stocks; nothing drawn changes the game; `prefers-reduced-motion` jumps per tick. The gardener's card (the panel) shows the day's hours ticking down and the job in hand.
  - **The `garden` check** (a browser group in `tools/checks/`): a seeded game with the default plan shows the gardener sow both beds and water them on day 1, shoots by day 2 or 3, and a first harvest carried to the kitchen by about day 8; the Garden tab's plan changes what the gardener does next. The scripted first minute (the first card, the goal bar, the beer trap) is part 6's; this check covers what this part draws.
  - **Saves before the first release (the owner, 29 Sep 2026).** Most of the game is built before it's released, so until the first release (part 15) saves carry no compatibility promise: change the saved shape freely, with no migration step and no test that an older build's save loads; a save the game can't read starts a new game, as today. Reshape earlier parts' code where this part needs it rather than working round it. Record the decision in this PR: a decision record in `docs/decisions/` (no save compatibility before the first release; from part 15 on, the rule "never rename or remove a saved field" holds with a migration per version), and edit the lines it makes untrue: the project notes' saved-state rule, the `feature` playbook's saved-state line, `docs/systems/saving.md` and `docs/SYSTEMS.md` ("State"). Part 2's migration step can stay or go, whichever is simpler.
  - **Tests.** A plausibility test per model (`<name>.test.ts` beside it): lettuce reaches harvest in about the RHS's weeks in a mild spring; a dry spell with no watering cuts yield in rough proportion to Ky; frost kills beans but not radishes; a bed cropped for a year draws its nitrogen down; the kitchen's need is met by two beds in summer and not in April; composting stores most of its carbon and emits the rest. `conservation`, the long headless run (which now grows food) and the `scene` and `layout` checks keep passing.
- **Pacing to aim at** (the spec's proposed targets, which part 4's bot will measure): first harvest on day 7 or 8 with the default plan; half the kitchen's need met within a few weeks. Tune constants only inside their sources' rough sizes, and report what the pacing came to.
- **It may touch:** `src/sim/models/` (the crops, kitchen and carbon models and their tests), `src/sim/` (the gardener's file, `systems.ts` entries, `commands.ts` for the plan, `state.ts`, `graph.ts` and `save.ts` as this part needs them, and earlier parts' code where it must be reshaped), `src/data/` (crops, job times, the kitchen's mix and price), `src/sim/models/water.ts` only for the crop coefficient hook, `src/ui/` (the Garden and Kitchen tabs, the gardener's card, the map's drawing of crops, the gardener and produce, `tokens.css`), `tools/checks/` (the new `garden` group, and `scene` or `layout` where this changes what they see), `docs/systems/` (a note each for crops, the gardener, the kitchen and carbon, naming every file in the first paragraph, and edits to water's), `docs/SYSTEMS.md` outside its joined lists, the save decision's record and the lines it makes untrue (above), `docs/roadmap.d/` (its own item), `docs/briefs/crops-and-gardener.md` (this brief, saved as is), `docs/lessons/` (its look back) and `src/updates.d/` (a What's new fragment). Not `tools/bot.ts`, `tools/baseline.json` or the Balance workflow: those are part 4's. No new runtime dependency. Anything else is outside the brief.
